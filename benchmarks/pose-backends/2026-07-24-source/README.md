These two files are immutable provenance for the adjacent
`windows-x64-{mediapipe-lite,rtmo-s}-cpu.json` observations. They were recovered
from reviewed baseline `15806d3c61c7fe79d04bb3c31f6964905e9bf7ae`, and their raw
SHA-256 digests exactly match both reports. They are evidence snapshots, not
the supported benchmark environment. Current commands and the single OpenCV
provider fix live in `experiments/rtmo` and `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`.

The original measurements, timestamps, source commits and environment claims
remain unchanged. Do not describe them as measurements of the current source,
nor update their hashes to accept an environment that did not produce them.
