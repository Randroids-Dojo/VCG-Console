# Owner questions: fixed-angle escalation evidence - 2026-07-26

Status: non-blocking for plan validation; blocking for fixed-baseline failure
import, alternative implementation or operation, participant work, mechanism
comparison, selection, purchase, BOM change, setup-guidance change or a
superseding decision

Safe default: D-092 remains unchanged. Grant no room, camera, participant,
construction, fabrication, purchase, operation or publication authority.

## FAE-001: admissible fixed-baseline failure

Which completed cross-tier geometry result and review can establish that zero
fixed integrated pitches qualify? Must every geometric, blocking-action,
calibration and setup-error cell be complete before escalation?

Safe default: require a complete result with every failed, stopped, invalid and
not-run cell retained. Null, incomplete, invalid or selectively rerun evidence
does not establish failure.

## FAE-002: exact failure ledger and retest policy

Which immutable ledger identifies every fixed-placement failure configuration,
persona, zone, motion, margin, calibration and uncertainty? When is a retest
allowed for a proven harness defect?

Safe default: bind a closed failure ledger before alternative work. Permit a
retest only under a separately reviewed harness-defect record and never delete
or overwrite the original observation.

## FAE-003: software auto-framing boundary

What exact local crop, scale, pan, player-change and floor-preservation behavior
defines software auto-framing? Which controls are automatic, disclosed,
cancelable and persisted?

Safe default: no network inference, no expanded capture authority, no hidden
crop or aim change, no floor/body clipping, and no use before an exact
implementation plus security/privacy review is bound.

## FAE-004: bounded manual mechanism

What exact detent range, increment, lock, force, wear, fastener, cable,
shutter/indicator clearance, stability and calibration-invalidation behavior
defines the smallest manual candidate?

Safe default: no free-sliding or silently movable mechanism. Require bounded
locking positions, ordinary-fastener service, safe cable routing and explicit
recalibration after every position change.

## FAE-005: motorized last-resort admission

What complete rejection evidence for both software auto-framing and bounded
manual tilt is required before motorized aim may enter testing? Who may
authorize its separate safety review?

Safe default: incomplete, not-run, inconvenient or more expensive
nonmotorized evidence does not admit motorization. Require both candidates to
fail the same frozen functional and fixed evidence gates first.

## FAE-006: candidate implementations and common comparator

Which exact received camera, enclosure, target, room envelope and candidate
hardware/software revisions must all mechanisms share? Which differences are
intrinsic to a mechanism and which invalidate comparison?

Safe default: bind one comparator configuration and separately digest every
intrinsic difference. A camera, lens, tracker, room, persona protocol or action
gate change requires a new frozen campaign.

## FAE-007: numeric functional gates

Freeze minimum head, feet and zone margins, maximum crop and floor error,
minimum per-cell action precision/recall, maximum correction and recalibration
time, and valid-trial/invalid-attempt rules.

Safe default: preserve the existing blocking action gates and keep all new
numeric gates null until justified independently of candidate results.

## FAE-008: privacy, disclosure and capture authority

How must aim/crop changes, capture activity, physical shutter state, visible
indicator, player switching and stop/revoke behavior be disclosed and
independently verified for each mechanism?

Safe default: camera state, indicator, shutter and aim/crop are separate facts.
Any undisclosed change, capture outside explicit authority, false privacy
belief or network egress is a failure.

## FAE-009: manual reach and accessibility

Who is expected to adjust a manual mechanism, from which supported positions,
with what reach, force, time, assistance, stability and unsafe-zone limits?

Safe default: do not require a child or player to climb, reach behind a TV,
enter the active zone while another person is playing, move furniture or
handle a live cable. Keep persona evidence separate.

## FAE-010: motor, pinch, obstruction and unexpected motion

Which calibrated obstruction, pinch/entrapment, stall, runaway, position-loss,
unexpected-motion, emergency-stop and fail-safe tests are required if
motorization becomes admissible?

Safe default: zero injury, unsafe force, uncontrolled movement or capture/aim
ambiguity. A power cut or control failure must fail stationary and require
explicit recovery.

## FAE-011: noise, power and thermal gates

Freeze acoustic measurement positions and ambient floor, maximum event and
sustained sound, idle/active/peak energy, thermal rise, power-cut behavior and
measurement uncertainty.

Safe default: keep thresholds null. Measure all candidates under the same
room, load and instrumentation; a mean cannot hide worst-case events.

## FAE-012: thickness, mass, cost and manufacturing evidence

Which enclosure-thickness and mass deltas, part/fastener counts, tooling,
assembly, yield, delivered BOM cost, warranty and repair-cost model must be
bound? What quote date, volume and tax/shipping boundary applies?

Safe default: require dated source-bound ranges and uncertainty. Catalog list
price, a prototype part, or a single optimistic quote cannot qualify a
production burden.

## FAE-013: reliability, update and service protocol

Freeze adjustment/motion cycle counts, environmental conditions, vibration,
wear, drift, reboot, suspend, crash, disconnect, update/rollback, recalibration,
repair and replacement rules.

Safe default: every failed, jammed, drifting, stale, stopped or invalid cycle
remains visible. Later recovery cannot erase a failure and replacement always
requires explicit recalibration.

## FAE-014: participants, data and publication

Approve the persona, consent/assent, guardian, accessibility, safety, rest,
withdrawal, adverse-event, minimized-data, retention, deletion and independent
review protocols. Who may publish a comparison?

Safe default: no participants and no raw room media, portraits or audio.
Release only path-free closed-vocabulary aggregates after deletion and privacy
review.

## FAE-015: pre-result decision rule

If multiple nonmotorized mechanisms repair every failure and pass all gates,
what pre-result rule ranks privacy, safety, reliability, usability, thickness,
power, noise and cost without hiding a failed domain?

Safe default: no weighted rescue. First reject any candidate with a failed or
missing fixed domain, then present the Pareto set and worst-case evidence for a
separate owner decision.

## FAE-016: superseding-decision authority

Who may select a mechanism, authorize purchase or BOM/setup changes, and record
a decision that supersedes D-092? What evidence must that decision cite?

Safe default: the campaign result is advisory only. No validator, aggregate,
agent or operator may change the product direction automatically.
