1. I-001 through I-007: freeze the room, representative games, body mechanic, player milestone, and success criteria.
2. I-003 and I-013: use owned hardware to establish the development baseline.
3. I-033, I-034, I-049, I-050, I-052, and I-053: prove single-player capture and pose in the real room.

6. I-004, I-089 through I-094: prove the launcher and representative current catalog.

8. Only then order or choose the embedded compute/camera tier using I-014 through I-020 and I-042.

## Closure rule

An investigation is not closed by a vendor claim, a one-time demo, or a URL returning 200. Close it with reproducible evidence, exact versions and hardware identifiers, observed failure cases, and the resulting decision update in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
