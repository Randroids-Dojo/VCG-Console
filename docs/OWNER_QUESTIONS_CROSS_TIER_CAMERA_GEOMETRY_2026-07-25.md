# Owner questions: cross-tier camera geometry — 2026-07-25

The campaign cannot become ready until these inputs are frozen. Safe defaults
grant no room access, participant recruitment, camera capture, furniture
movement, mounting, cutting, drilling, construction or purchasing authority.

## QCG-001: measured integrated grid values

What exact millimetre values define low/nominal/high enclosure height, shallow/
nominal/deep TV-stand depth, near/nominal/far player distance and narrow/
nominal/wide room width? What exact upward degrees define the five pitch roles?

Safe default: keep every value null. Derive them from the completed primary-room
survey plus a documented representative-envelope rationale before observations.

## QCG-002: fixed-pitch selection rule

If more than one integrated pitch passes, which pre-result rule selects the one
fixed axis—largest worst-cell margin, smallest enclosure impact, most robust
setup envelope, or another ordered rule?

Safe default: select the passing pitch with the largest minimum blocking-cell
head/feet/zone/floor margin, then use lower enclosure height and smaller crop
loss as pre-declared tie breakers. Do not select by appearance after results.

## QCG-003: exact integrated prototype

Which received camera revision, retained housing/clip/shutter/indicator/cable,
ABS box, adjustable jig, fasteners, feet and cable restraints define the
integrated screening fixture?

Safe default: wait for I-177/I-196 and received-part measurements. Catalog
dimensions and the current box lead do not authorize cutting or establish fit.

## QCG-004: external targets and mount assemblies

Which supported-PC and Steam Machine hardware/software tuples are blocking,
and which exact stand/clip/fastener/cable assembly implements each of the five
external mount roles?

Safe default: require distinct exact tuples and mount identities. If Steam
hardware is unavailable, retain its rows as not run; do not promote PC evidence.

## QCG-005: optical and floor gates

What minimum horizontal/vertical field of view and head/feet/zone-edge margins,
and what maximum distortion, crop loss and floor-plane error, must every
blocking cell satisfy?

Safe default: keep all gates null until product geometry, independent prior
evidence and measurement uncertainty justify them. Do not choose gates from
the campaign result.

## QCG-006: calibration and placement-change timing

What maximum calibration duration and placement-change detection time are
acceptable? Which changes must force full recalibration versus a bounded
verification step?

Safe default: any camera, mount, pitch, height, target, cable/port or room/floor
change invalidates calibration. Keep timing gates null until the user flow and
instrumented implementation exist.

## QCG-007: independent ground truth

Which calibrated optical target, floor reference and action-labeling method
independently establish field of view, body/floor margins and jump/duck/dodge
outcomes? Who operates and reviews them?

Safe default: bind separate protocols and reviewers. Candidate landmarks,
camera preview and calibration output cannot label their own correctness.

## QCG-008: mount stability, cable and USB protocol

What tip, slip, vibration, cable-pull, strain-relief, bend-radius, USB-length,
bandwidth/drop/reconnect and child/pet interaction procedures qualify each
mount and enclosure configuration?

Safe default: require zero stability/cable safety failures and zero capture
errors/drops, with calibrated forces and cycle counts chosen before testing.
Static CAD or dimensions cannot substitute for the physical checks.

## QCG-009: participants and exploratory cases

Which consent/assent/comprehension protocol authorizes adult and school-age-
child blocking trials, and may seated/limited-range volunteers participate as
exploratory evidence in the same schedule?

Safe default: schedule no participant until approved. Keep persona results
separate; exploratory evidence cannot rescue a blocking failure.

## QCG-010: redacted geometry evidence

Which redacted photographs or diagrams are necessary to reproduce camera datum,
mounting and sight lines, and who approves their capture, access and deletion?

Safe default: prefer dimensioned abstract diagrams and numeric/skeleton
artifacts. If photos are necessary, require separate authority, EXIF/GPS
removal, household redaction and deletion verification before release.

## QCG-011: external support publication rule

Should setup guidance advertise every passing external mount role or select one
preferred role plus measured fallbacks? How should rejected roles be shown?

Safe default: publish one preferred passing role per target tier, list every
other passing role as a measured alternative, and explicitly mark rejected or
untested roles unsupported without hiding their evidence.

## QCG-012: workload staging and stop rules

May the 405 integrated screen precede participant validation, and what safety,
consent, ground-truth or equipment failures stop the 11,000-trial phase?

Safe default: complete the nonparticipant geometric screen first, freeze the
selection, then run participant cells. Stop for safety, withdrawal, invalid
ground truth or equipment drift; retain every stopped/invalid cell and do not
replace it silently.
