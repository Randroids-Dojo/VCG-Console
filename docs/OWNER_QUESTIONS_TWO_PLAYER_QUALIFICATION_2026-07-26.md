# Owner questions: two-player qualification

Date: 2026-07-26
Scope: I-054
Status: decisions required before execution

These questions preserve the zero-result plan. Answering them does not by
itself authorize participants, room access, camera collection, runtime
mutation, qualification, or four-player work. Freeze every answer into an
exact reviewed protocol before any affected evidence is collected.

## TPQ-001 — One-player prerequisite closure

Which exact I-053 result and required target/persona/placement/action/negative
cells must pass before I-054 may begin, and who signs that closure? Synthetic
fixtures, aggregate scores, partial placements, or a different target cannot
substitute.

## TPQ-002 — Selected target and runtime tuple

Which one-player-qualified prototype target, OS image, kernel, drivers,
compositor, browser, Motion runtime, shell build, obstacle build, and clean
configuration form the exact two-player target? Define invalidation and
retest triggers for every relevant change.

## TPQ-003 — Two-player tracker configuration

Which exact detector, tracker, model, runtime, parameters, confidence/loss
rules, provider mapping, and artifact digests run the campaign? State which
Q-028/Q-029 evidence selects it and forbid tuning after outcomes are visible.

## TPQ-004 — Camera, calibration, and common clock

Which qualified camera/mode/placement/capture policy, separate per-player
calibration protocol, exposure timestamp, Motion delivery clock, and
uncertainty budget apply? Capture arrival and inference completion cannot
stand in for exposure time.

## TPQ-005 — Room and paired placements

Which measured room result, safe two-player subzones, exact marks, movement
envelopes, spacing, collision clearance, and camera visibility define the
five placement pairs? Specify what room/furniture/camera movement invalidates
evidence.

## TPQ-006 — Participant-pair cohort

What minimum number of independent pairs is required for each ordered
child/child, child/adult, adult/child, and adult/adult class? Define repeat
participation, exclusions, withdrawals, replacements, and why the cohort is
adequate without letting one class rescue another.

## TPQ-007 — Consent, assent, safety, and stop rules

Approve adult consent, guardian consent, child assent, instruction,
supervision, fatigue/rest, clothing, collision, distress, withdrawal,
incident, and emergency-stop protocols. Who may stop a trial, and how are
stopped/adverse attempts retained without pressure to continue?

## TPQ-008 — Independent identity oracle

Which non-tracker ground truth identifies P1/P2 through crossing, occlusion,
depth exchange, exit, and re-entry with bounded uncertainty? Define switch,
fragmentation, false transfer, ambiguous truth, scorer blinding, and
adjudication without storing names, faces, or durable identity.

## TPQ-009 — Action and ownership oracle

How are expected per-player Dodge/Duck/Jump, delivered player slot,
privileged actions, launcher owner, overlay owner, focus movement, and pause
race completion independently timestamped and scored? Define simultaneous
and ambiguous events before trials.

## TPQ-010 — Runtime freeze and state integrity

What exact shell and obstacle-sample adapters prove that simulation, timers,
hazards, scoring, and motion actions do not advance after either player's
confirmed loss? Freeze the pre/during/post state digest, observation points,
resume oracle, and behavior on adapter failure.

## TPQ-011 — Accessible player identities

Approve the exact number, pattern/shape, color, size, contrast, animation,
audio/text alternatives, viewing distance, language, teach-back, and
controller/motion paths. What comprehension percentage qualifies each task,
and what assistance invalidates a trial?

## TPQ-012 — Join, loss, recovery, and pause timing

Freeze the maximum sequential join/calibration time, ordinary loss
confirmation deadline, recovery-overlay readiness deadline, and pause-owner

choosing thresholds after results.

## TPQ-013 — Identity and pose gates

Freeze minimum per-player pose FPS and core-17 coverage, maximum jitter and

## TPQ-014 — Performance and resource gates

Freeze maximum CPU, GPU, resident memory, wall power, and temperature plus

throttling, drops, and fault handling.

## TPQ-015 — Schedule, repetitions, and operators

Approve the randomized/counterbalanced schedule for 4,800 identity trials,

retests, abandonment, and the prohibition on replacing product failures.

## TPQ-016 — Data handling and temporary diagnostics
