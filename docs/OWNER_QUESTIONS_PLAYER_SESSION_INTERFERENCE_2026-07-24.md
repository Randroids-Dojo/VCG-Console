# Owner questions: player-session household interference

Last updated: 2026-07-24

These questions block physical I-069 qualification, not the deterministic
camera-free rehearsal. No answer is assumed by the implementation.

## Q-220: physical campaign and participant authorization

May the project schedule the 840-trial real-room player-session interference
campaign with consented school-age-child and adult standing participants?

Needed before scheduling:

- selected room and safe zone;
- exact target-Linux appliance, camera, mount, tracker, and controller;
- adult consent and child assent/guardian consent process;
- named responsible operator and independent stop observer;
- session duration, breaks, compensation, and data-deletion timing;
- permission to include a consented replacement player in recovery scenes.

Default while unanswered: no participant recruitment, recording, or physical
campaign execution.

## Q-221: exact Resume selection ceremony

When more than one candidate is visible in one-player recovery, what is the
product-approved way to direct Resume to exactly one candidate?

Candidate options that need a decision and usability evidence:

1. controller focus moves across redundant candidate shapes/numbers, followed
   by explicit confirmation;
2. the intended candidate performs a dedicated recovery gesture while a
   controller remains available;
3. the previous player confirms a named replacement candidate;
4. require all other candidates to leave the zone before Resume becomes
   available.

The current state machine accepts an exact selected track and never chooses
one internally. The synthetic UI does not qualify a production selection
ceremony. Default: never select the first detection or array element
implicitly.

## Q-222: pet and animal-welfare scope

Is a live household pet required for the blocking campaign, and if so, which
species and ordinary unprompted behaviors may be observed?

Needed constraints:

- no restraint, baiting, startling, forced repetition, or unsafe placement;
- owner/handler present and able to stop immediately;
- maximum session duration and rest;
- conditions that require replacing the live-pet cell with an incomplete
  result rather than a staged substitute;
- whether multiple pet sizes/coats are required.

Default: do not acquire, borrow, handle, direct, or provoke an animal for
testing. A television video of an animal is a display fixture, not live-pet
evidence.

## Q-223: mirror and television fixture boundaries

Which exact mirror geometries and television content belong in the first
blocking matrix?

Needed minimums:

- full-length versus partial mirror, angle, distance, and overlap with the
  player;
- television size, brightness, content resolution, person scale, motion,
  camera cuts, and player/television overlap;
- whether video calls, picture-in-picture, and paused person images are
  separate cases;
- whether glass doors or dark reflective televisions count as mirrors.

Default: the plan must bind one exact full-body mirror script and one exact
moving-person television script; broader claims remain unavailable.

## Q-224: repetition floor and reliability claim

Is twelve scheduled repetitions with at least ten valid trials per each of 70
cells sufficient for the first qualification claim?

The current contract schedules 840 trials and requires at least 700 valid,
with zero false joins, controls, unintended takeovers, or false actions. This
is a pre-registration floor, not a statistically justified consumer
reliability claim.

Please decide whether the intended public claim requires:

- more repetitions;
- multiple days;
- multiple rooms or lighting conditions;
- multiple cameras or hardware units;
- confidence intervals or an upper failure-rate bound;
- an independent replication operator.

Default: use the current floor only for the selected room/configuration and
make no generalized household reliability claim.

## Q-225: child/adult and later accessibility scope

Should a failure in either blocking standing persona reject the entire first
configuration, and when do seated, assisted, and limited-range cases become
blocking?

Current contract:

- school-age-child standing and adult standing are separate mandatory cells;
- one valid failure in either rejects the campaign;
- results cannot be pooled to hide a persona-specific failure;
- seated, assisted, and limited-range qualification remains I-068.

Default: both standing personas are blocking; accessibility variants remain
explicitly unqualified rather than inferred.

## Q-226: minimized evidence review and retention

Who may inspect the campaign's skeleton/event evidence, for how long, and what
must be deleted after scoring?

The validator prohibits raw-frame retention but cannot itself decide:

- retention duration for minimized skeleton/event artifacts;
- whether participant/session pseudonyms are needed at all;
- access reviewers and audit logging;
- encryption key ownership;
- incident/debug exception handling;
- deletion proof and derived-report retention;
- whether any artifact may leave the device.

Default: opaque run-local IDs only, device-local encrypted evidence, least
access, no cloud transfer, no raw frames, and delete minimized trial artifacts
after review while retaining only digests and aggregate reports.
