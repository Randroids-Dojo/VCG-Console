# Owner questions: DIY distribution readiness

Date: 2026-07-26

Scope: decisions deliberately withheld from the autonomous I-143/I-144 plan

No answer is assumed, and no external contact, household recruitment, testing,
filing, publication, sale, or support commitment has been authorized.

## Q-DDR-001: exact first release candidate

What exact tag, software images/packages, first-party and third-party content,
BOM, enclosure files, build instructions, and user-facing materials form the
first public candidate?

Freeze exact artifact identities, byte lengths, SHA-256 values, supported
hardware revisions, excluded substitutions, and one change-control rule. Until
then applicability and release-material reviews have no stable subject.

## Q-DDR-002: operator, business model, and channels

Who is the legal operator/publisher, and will the project remain source-only
and noncommercial with no kits, assembled hardware, paid services, warranty,
or formal support?

Record operator identity outside public evidence where necessary, exact release
channels, donations/sponsorship facts, contributor relationships, and any role
held by a repository host, package host, image mirror, or game/service operator.
Any hardware sale, kit, paid service, or supported-product promise reopens the
scope before release.

## Q-DDR-003: target markets

Which countries, states/provinces, languages, and distribution channels are
intentionally targeted, and how should globally accessible repository files be
treated?

Qualified review must decide the applicability scope. The plan does not infer
that source publication is outside every market or that a DIY/no-warranty label
creates an exemption.

## Q-DDR-004: qualified reviewers and conflicts

Who independently owns electrical, radio, optical-emission, thermal/fire,
consumer-product/instruction, privacy, accessibility, open-source license, and
general legal review?

Freeze qualifications, independence/conflict rules, review-record format,
adverse-evidence handling, escalation, source-currency checks, and the point at
which a changed candidate invalidates prior review. No reviewer has been
contacted by this tranche.

## Q-DDR-005: first-party licenses

Which exact licenses and copyright-holder wording apply to code/build scripts,
documentation/safe-play materials, and enclosure/CAD/templates?

This carries forward Q-RC-001. Supply exact license texts/SPDX expressions,
inbound contribution policy, compatibility review, attribution, notices, and
source obligations before the release gate can pass.

## Q-DDR-006: model, game, font, firmware, and content rights

What exact evidence authorizes every redistributed model, game, asset, font,
firmware/core, operating-system image component, and third-party binary in the
candidate?

This includes the unresolved model-specific terms for the pinned
`pose_landmarker_lite.task` artifact from Q-RC-002. Package or repository
licenses cannot silently authorize separately downloaded artifacts. Omit or
replace anything without exact compatible rights.

## Q-DDR-007: hardware and substitution boundary

Which exact received hardware assembly and supported revisions may the build
instructions describe, and which builder substitutions are allowed, rejected,
or explicitly unqualified?

Freeze power supplies, cables, storage, radios, camera/IR sources, cooling,
enclosure material/cuts, fasteners, mounts, and intended use. Supplier
declarations and component certifications do not automatically qualify the
assembled reference or an arbitrary substitution.

## Q-DDR-008: home-pilot safety and comprehension

What authority, households, school-age-child/adult sampling, consent/assent,
room constraints, stopping rules, issue handling, privacy boundary, and
pre-registered comprehension gates govern the home-pilot review?

Do not recruit or collect evidence until approved. A no-incident session cannot
be promoted into a general safety claim, and consent cannot waive a failed
physical gate.

## Q-DDR-009: target-TV accessibility review

What blocking child/adult, low-vision, color-vision, reading/comprehension,
controller-only, hearing/audio-cue, reduced-motion, and assistive-technology
protocol applies to the eight material lanes on both reference tiers?

Freeze languages, seating distance, TV/display/audio path, participant scope,
sample rules, severity policy, and issue closure before testing. Browser desk
screenshots or engineering review cannot substitute for this evidence.

## Q-DDR-010: privacy and data-flow approval

Which exact markets, operator roles, ages, services, data flows, retention/
deletion behavior, notices, lawful bases/consent paths, vendor agreements,
incident processes, and product claims receive qualified privacy/legal review?

The existing child-privacy brief is an engineering input, not approval. Keep
portraits and persistent automatic body-profile matching outside a networked
family beta until their separate accuracy, security, consent, deletion, and
legal gates pass.

## Q-DDR-011: security, incident, and support ownership

What public security-reporting channel, response owner, severity/embargo rule,
update/removal commitment, known-risk register, incident routing, and explicit
support boundary will ship?

Avoid promising response times or support capacity that do not exist. The
chosen process must not require publication of household, child, credential,
device, network, path, support-bundle, or raw media data.

## Q-DDR-012: warranty, claims, signoff, and publication authority

What reviewed no-warranty/no-formal-support wording, safe-use limitations,
compatibility statements, privacy/accessibility claims, final signoff format,
candidate freeze window, and source-currency window are required?

Who may give the separate final owner approval to publish after every domain,
all 32 material-review cells, the release-compliance gate, and every fixed-zero
gate pass? This plan cannot supply or infer that approval.
