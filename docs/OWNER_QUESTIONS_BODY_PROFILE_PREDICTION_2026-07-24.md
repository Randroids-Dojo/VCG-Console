# Owner questions: automatic body-profile prediction

The camera-free contract can prove conservative software behavior, but it
cannot choose whether household body recognition should exist or define the
human, legal, and product evidence required to enable it.

## Q-240: product authority and qualification campaign

Should automatic device-local body-profile prediction proceed at all; if so,
which exact product purpose, launch jurisdictions, adult/child and
accessibility cohorts, room/camera/clothing/change-over-time conditions,
feature extractor and model-selection process, household notice/consent and
opt-out wording, visible prediction/correction UI, false-accept,
false-reject, abstention, and correction gates, independent reviewers, and
target assemblies authorize its qualification?

Safe default: keep matching absent from family and release builds. Preserve
explicit profile selection, New Player, and transient calibration as complete
paths. Do not collect or persist a template, expose a profile name or
portrait from an unconfirmed prediction, call matching authentication, use
portrait/face/appearance inputs, aggregate away cohort failures, or enable
matching until the encrypted-vault, protected-state, deletion/reset,
negative-leak, legal/privacy, security, accessibility, household-usability,
and both-target gates all pass.
