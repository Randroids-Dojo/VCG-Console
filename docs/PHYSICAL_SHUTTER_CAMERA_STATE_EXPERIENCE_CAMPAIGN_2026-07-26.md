# Physical shutter and camera-state experience campaign

Date: 2026-07-26

Status: strict zero-result I-046 plan. No camera or enclosure selection,
purchase, receipt, power, capture, shutter cycling, indicator observation,
idle or suspend injection, participant collection, product-surface change,
result, qualification, publication, or product claim is authorized.

## Claim boundary

`benchmarks/camera-state/physical-shutter-camera-state-experience-plan-v1.json`
pre-registers the remaining physical half of I-046 without weakening the
implemented software truth boundary. Software access, capture-stream activity,
hardware capture indication, and physical shutter position remain four
separate facts.

The current software reports the physical shutter as `NOT SENSED` in every
state. An active stream cannot prove an open shutter or useful image. A black
frame, missing landmarks, stopped tracker, idle UI, permission denial, process
failure, disconnected cable, or extinguished software badge cannot prove a
closed shutter or unpowered camera.

The plan covers three independent target-package roles: ordinary x86 Linux
with an external camera, SteamOS with an external camera, and the Raspberry Pi
5 appliance with an integrated camera. One target, camera, enclosure, capture
path, state, lighting condition, UI surface, persona, distance, angle, or later
recovery cannot rescue another.

## Participant-free shutter and indicator bench

The optical matrix crosses all three target roles, operator-verified open and
closed shutter positions, four lighting states, and three calibrated geometric
fixtures. It contains 72 cells and 1,440 ordered observations at twenty valid
observations per cell. The fixtures separately probe center resolution,
perimeter leakage, and a moving geometric target. Independent shutter-position
and optical instruments are required; image darkness cannot label shutter
position or optical blocking.

Four shutter transitions—open to closed, closed to open, active capture to
closed, and active capture to open—run fifty cycles per target role for 600
cycles. Every jam, partial coverage, rebound, wear, force event, indicator
contradiction, invalid observation, stop, and retry remains visible.

The hardware/software indicator matrix crosses three target roles, four
capture-path roles, twelve lifecycle states, and four room-lighting states. It
contains 576 cells and 11,520 observations. Each observation independently
records OS/device capture evidence, application software state, software
activity copy, hardware indicator state, indicator electrical evidence, and
physical shutter position without inferring one fact from another.

The twelve states include disabled, backend starting, permission requesting,
capture active, permission denied, unavailable, active-device disconnect,
tracker failure, explicit stop complete, idle timeout complete, host suspended,
and resume awaiting explicit restart. Only independently proven capture-active
observations may show `STREAM ACTIVE`; all other registered software states
show `NO STREAM`. The physical shutter remains `NOT SENSED` throughout.

Eight lifecycle transitions across every target and capture-path role add 96
cells and 1,920 cycles. They cover explicit stop, idle timeout, suspend,
resume-without-auto-restart, explicit restart, disconnect, reconnect without
auto-restart, and process-crash release. Capture and its hardware activity
indicator must end fail-closed; an automatic resume or stale active indication
is a failure.

Forty-eight separate fifteen-minute capture-absent negative sessions cover
every target, capture-path role, and lighting state. Unexpected camera access,
indicator activity, stream activity, frames, auto-retry, or network egress is a
failure and cannot be averaged away.

## Product surfaces and human evidence remain blocked

Before physical participants, a camera-free render matrix covers the Motion
Lab camera card, a setup/settings camera surface, and a pause/recovery camera

That is 72 render cells. Layout conformance cannot establish comprehension,

The registered comprehension phase crosses five persona classes, all three UI
surface roles, eight software states, two viewing distances, three viewing
angles, and four lighting conditions: 2,880 cells and 57,600 ordered trials at
twenty valid trials per cell. Each uncoached trial asks separately what
software access says, whether a stream is active, whether the physical shutter

A separate reach and visibility matrix crosses five persona classes, three

operating the shutter, reaching the connector or service release, and using
non-motion recovery without entering an unsafe play or beam path.

The five persona classes are school-age child standing, adult standing,
seated or limited-range adult, low-vision adult, and color-vision-deficiency
adult. These are sampling roles, not diagnoses or population claims. Exact
participants, consent/assent, guardian authority, accessibility protocol,
room, television, lighting, distances, compensation, privacy, rest, stop,
deletion, and adverse-event handling remain absent.

## Fail-closed evidence and data policy

The campaign requires zero active capture without the qualified hardware
activity indicator, zero indicator-off observations during active capture,
zero false software stream indication, zero software claims that the shutter
is open or closed, zero automatic capture restart after idle, suspend,
disconnect, or crash, and zero hidden invalid or stopped trials.

All optical attenuation, leakage, indicator luminance and contrast, viewing
angle, state-transition time, capture-release time, shutter force and wear,
comprehension, recovery accuracy, reach time, and accessibility gates remain
null. They must be approved before operation and cannot be authored from
results.

Release evidence is path-free closed-vocabulary aggregate data only. Raw
camera frames, room images, audio, faces, portraits, names, exact ages,
addresses, stable identifiers, device serials, filesystem paths, credentials,
provider messages, and free text are forbidden. Temporary diagnostics require
separate authority, security, consent where applicable, and verified deletion.
