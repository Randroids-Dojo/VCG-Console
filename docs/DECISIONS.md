## Decision process

1. Every material architecture or product commitment receives an ID.
2. The investigation that validates or invalidates it is linked from [INVESTIGATIONS.md](INVESTIGATIONS.md).
3. A change preserves the old row and adds a dated superseding decision rather than rewriting history.
4. Hardware decisions record exact part revision, price quote date, firmware, OS image, model, benchmark artifact, and room setup.
5. A decision is not accepted merely because a demo works once. Its stated exit evidence must be reproducible.
