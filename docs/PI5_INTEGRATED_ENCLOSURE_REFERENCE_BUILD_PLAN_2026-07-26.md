# Pi 5 integrated enclosure and reference-build plan — 2026-07-26

Status: strict blocked I-192/I-195 zero-result plan; no enclosure has been
selected, purchased, cut, assembled, operated, photographed, reproduced, or
published

Authority: D-043, D-044, D-045, D-090 through D-095, D-102, D-103, D-108,
D-110, D-111, I-192, and I-195

## Outcome

Pre-register the complete evidence boundary for the first secured below-TV
Raspberry Pi enclosure and the later reproducible DIY reference build. The
tracked machine-readable artifact is:

`benchmarks/enclosure/pi5-integrated-enclosure-reference-build-plan-v1.json`

This tranche creates a plan and validator only. It does not turn the existing
ABS market screen into a selection, authorize a transaction or construction,
or represent upstream camera, room, cooling, radio, power, cable, service, or
cost plans as physical results.

The current `WA-40*16` lead remains only the least-constrained catalog
candidate for CAD and cardboard fit. Approximate manufacturer dimensions,
STEP geometry, bare-board rectangles, price before shipping and tax, or a
successful cardboard assembly cannot prove received fit, safety, delivered
cost, optical coverage, thermals, acoustics, radio, service, or operation.

## Product and prototype boundary

D-090 and D-094 require the lower-cost prototype to contain Raspberry Pi
compute and the shared RGB camera in one modified off-the-shelf ABS project
box below the television. D-091 does not impose this packaging on Steam Machine
or ordinary-PC targets.

D-092 permits an adjustable camera test jig only to discover the production
axis. Before any qualifying evidence is collected, the primary build must lock
one fixed pitch. The independent reproduction uses that same fixed datum and
jig. Manual or motorized adjustment is not a qualifying production feature.

D-093 keeps the project in a DIY, non-warranty service posture. Ordinary
reversible fasteners and a standard camera connector are required where
practical, but the plan does not claim formal consumer-product serviceability,
tamper resistance, ingress protection, certification, or warranty support.

## Two required specimens

The evidence ledger has two non-rescuing specimens:

- `primary-i192-integrated-prototype` may use adjustable pitch during discovery
  but must be mechanically locked to the selected fixed axis for every
  qualifying result; and
- `independent-i195-reference-reproduction` must be built by a different
  builder using the frozen source package only, with no adjustable discovery,
  oral corrections, undocumented shims, pre-cut hidden parts, or first-build
  team intervention.

Each specimen binds its own received box revision and dimensions, complete
component/BOM manifest, mounts, fasteners, feet, vents, guards, strain relief,
tools, builder environment, safety packet and fixed-axis datum. Both evidence
ledgers remain separate. The first build cannot rescue the reproduction, and
the reproduction cannot hide the first build's failures.

At least one independent builder and one complete independent reproduction are
mandatory. More samples, repetitions, wear cycles and fault cycles remain an
owner-reviewed open gate before physical work.

## Frozen design package

Before the first cut, one versioned package must include:

- editable source dimensions and a calibrated one-to-one cut template;
- measured component, connector, cable-bend, boss, rib and fastener keep-outs;
- tolerance stacks for the received enclosure and installed assemblies;
- camera optical datum, fixed-axis jig and allowed pitch/roll/yaw error;
- fastener engagement and torque, feet, center-of-gravity, tip, slip, vent,
  guard, probe and deburring specifications;
- power, HDMI, USB and camera routing plus independent strain relief;
- physical shutter, capture-indicator, wake, restart, shutdown, port and
  ordinary-fastener service access;
- cooling airflow, radio keep-outs and instrument placement;
- assembly, disassembly, inspection, recalibration, fault and recovery steps;
  and
- exact BOM, delivered cost, tools, consumables, redacted-photo rules,
  versioning, licensing and publication contents.

Modifying a catalog box invalidates any inherited ingress or safety rating
unless separately supported. Undocumented dimensions, tools or adjustments
make the reproduction invalid rather than becoming informal instructions.

## Complete stage matrix

Both specimens must complete all twelve ordered stages:

1. receipt identity, dimensions, material and delivered cost;
2. measured component stack, connector keep-outs and tolerance model;
3. editable template, camera datum, fixed-axis jig and source review;
4. one-to-one paper/cardboard fit, assembly order and collision review;
5. cut, edge, deburr, fastener, vent, guard and rating inspection;
6. mounted assembly, tip, slip, cable pull and strain relief;
7. shutter, indicator, wake, power and ordinary-fastener service access;
8. fixed-axis room coverage, calibration, floor contact and action behavior;
9. enclosed thermal, acoustic, power and sustained complete workload;
10. closed-enclosure Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, camera and controller coexistence;
11. shutdown, idle, wake, disconnect, fault recovery and recalibration; and
12. disassembly, reassembly, cost, household safety and independent review.

This creates 24 required specimen-stage cells. Every cell must be present and
valid under the same frozen source package, BOM, axis, gates, oracles and result
schema. Failed, invalid, stopped, retried, reworked and pre-repair first-failure
evidence remains visible.

## Household and mechanical safety matrix

Each specimen also runs sixteen explicit safety scenarios, creating 32
required specimen-scenario cells. The scenarios cover:

- child finger/probe and reach behavior around openings and controls;
- pet contact and cable snag;
- walking trip loops and every external cable route;
- four-direction tip load, sliding, cable pull and connector bend;
- burrs, swarf, sharp edges, exposed fan blades, hot parts and conductors;
- loose fasteners, small parts, pinch/shear and tool slip;
- blocked vents, fan fault and predefined stop response;
- shutter, indicator, wake and power access without unsafe-zone entry;
- normal furniture, doors, egress and preservation of the required play zone;
  and
- emergency stop, power isolation and safe recovery.

Independent physical ground truth and stop rules are required. Lack of injury,
successful boot, good signal quality, lower cost or any aggregate cannot
establish safety or rescue a failed safety cell.

The enclosure cannot weaken upstream product gates. Both specimens retain:

- zero missing required stage or safety cells;
- zero burr, sharp-edge, loose-swarf, unsecured-fastener, accessible hot,
  moving or conductive, unstrain-relieved-cable, tip, slip, snag, trip, pinch,
  egress, unsafe-zone, privacy, shutter, indicator, wake, vent, service,
  camera, radio, controller or recovery failures;

- D-110's 120,000 microsecond exposure-to-game-API p95 ceiling;
- the measured 2438.4 by 2438.4 mm one/two-player zone;
- D-108's 35 dBA one-metre enclosure ceiling;
- the camera-service campaign's five-minute complete-service ceiling and at
  least twenty valid disassembly/reassembly/recalibration attempts per
  specimen;

- frozen samples, thresholds, protocols, oracles and stop rules before any
  physical work.

Outcome-sensitive enclosure cost, complete-system cost, dimensions, mass, tip
angle, slip force, cable retention, displacement, template and axis tolerance,
guard clearance, probe openings, temperature, throttle/fan behavior, power,
radio regression, wake timing, assembly/service time, assistance, wear,
samples and expiry/retest policy remain null for owner review. They cannot be
chosen after observing results.

## Reproduction and publication boundary

After a complete primary pass, the exact BOM, template, fixed-axis jig,
instructions, gates and result schema are frozen into one digested candidate
package. A different builder receives only that package and must complete the
same matrix. Any needed oral fix, undocumented shim, first-team intervention or
source correction requires a new version and restart of affected evidence.

Neither technical pass automatically selects the box, camera axis, BOM or
publication disposition. A complete reproduction does not create a warranty,
compliance, safety-certification, compatibility or support claim. Publication
requires both complete passing results, independent safety/technical/cost
review and a separate owner decision covering version, license, photo
redaction, support boundary, expiry and retest.

## Data boundary

Repository results use opaque specimen, builder, session, stage, scenario,
trial, fault and reason labels plus closed counts, timings, digests,
dimensions, metrics and costs. They exclude raw room media, faces, bodies,
children, pets, portraits, camera frames, names, addresses, receipts, orders,
payment/tax data, seller or builder identities, serials, MAC addresses, Wi-Fi
names, credentials, hostnames, usernames, paths, environment values and free
text logs.

Build photos require separate authority, no people or home-identifying details,
redaction and metadata removal. Failed and adverse evidence may not be hidden.
