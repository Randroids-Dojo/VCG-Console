# Owner questions: cross-device IR interference and safety

Date: 2026-07-26

Safe defaults authorize no device operation, emitter/flood/external-IR use,
sunlight or reflective staging, participant collection, fault injection,
result publication, selection, purchase, or BOM change.

## IR-001: received devices and exact modes

Confirm the exact received OAK-D Pro W `A00573`, Gemini 335L `G40055-170`, and
D455 `82635DSD455` / `999WCT` units, revisions, firmware, optical filters,
enclosures, cables, mounts, calibrations, projector/flood parts, and private
serial digests. Must every one of the seven registered modes remain blocking?

Safe default: require all seven without substitution. Reject damaged, opened,
modified, unofficially flashed, filtered, or mismatched devices.

## IR-002: emitter identity, wavelength, and controls

Bind official received-revision evidence for source type, nominal wavelength,
field, pulse behavior, intensity range, default, temperature limits, protection
features, and commanded-versus-observed state. What is the exact D455 nominal
projector wavelength?

Safe default: keep every active mode blocked, especially D455, until exact
documentation and an independent state observer exist.

## IR-003: legal, regulatory, and safety review

Which geography, distribution intent, laser/IR/electrical/child-product rules,
standards, counsel, compliance owner, lab, records, and stop authority govern
bench and participant operation?

Safe default: vendor Class 1 statements grant no project authority and no
distribution claim. Do not operate around people.

## IR-004: room, sun, and weather boundary

Select the exact room, window orientation/material, date/time or controlled
light source, solar geometry, illuminance/IR instruments, curtains, exclusion
zone, weather validity, and repeat schedule. May natural sun variability be
used, or is a reviewed simulator required?

Safe default: no deliberate direct-sun aiming, outdoor exposure, improvised
high-power source, or result-dependent condition selection.

## IR-005: reflective fixtures and beam paths

Freeze exact matte, TV, glass, mirror, metal, and glossy-floor materials,
dimensions, reflectance, incidence angles, mounts, barriers, beam envelopes,
operator location, and safe removal procedure.

Safe default: participant-free fixtures only; no person or animal in a direct
or specular path, no magnifying optics, and no unreviewed substitution.

## IR-006: other IR devices

Select exact same-model and cross-vendor cameras, 850 nm and 940 nm sources,
TV remote, night-vision illuminator, firmware, output, duty cycle, orientation,
separation, synchronization, power, and state observers.

Safe default: all external sources off. A category name cannot authorize an
unknown device or intensity.

## IR-007: presets, HDR, filters, and mitigations

Freeze exposure, gain, presets, HDR/alternation, confidence, post-processing,
projector/flood intensity, synchronization, optical filters, polarizers, and
warmup. May any mitigation be a separate registered lane?

Safe default: no silent mitigation or physical filter. A recovery configuration
cannot rewrite or rescue its failing source cell.

## IR-008: bench fixture and ground truth

Define the non-human target, calibrated geometry, reflectance targets, motion,
range, independent depth truth, saturation/fill/false-depth scoring, timing,
thermal/power instruments, uncertainty, operators, barriers, and invalid-run
rules.

Safe default: no depth self-labeling, screenshots as measurement, or aggregate
rescue. Keep all values null until independently reviewed.

## IR-009: transition, soak, and stop rules

Approve the eight condition transitions, twenty-one one-hour soaks, automatic
and manual stops, cooldown, emitter-off verification, healthy-return proof,
device quarantine, incident response, and whether stopped cells may ever
restart.

Safe default: every stop remains visible and makes the affected mode incomplete
or failed. Later recovery does not erase the event.

## IR-010: participant and child authority

Who may approve adults and school-age children after bench qualification?
Freeze consent/assent, guardians, medical/accessibility exclusions, exposure
limits, distances, movements, breaks, stop signals, compensation, privacy,
retention, deletion, and adverse-event handling.

Safe default: no human phase. Bench success is not consent or child-safety
evidence.

## IR-011: numeric gates

Freeze pre-result limits for irradiance/proxy, ambient visible/IR level,
temperature, power, saturation, valid-depth fill, false/missing depth,
instability, alignment, timestamps, pose/action accuracy, recovery, stops, and
minimum material benefit. Identify uncertainty and per-cell disposition rules.

Safe default: all open limits remain null; results cannot author them.

## IR-012: evidence and decision authority

Who may authorize operation, raw temporary diagnostics, result publication,
comparison conclusions, camera selection, purchase, or BOM change?

Safe default: nobody under this plan. Release only path-free closed-vocabulary
aggregates without images, point clouds, identities, serials, paths, secrets,
unredacted logs, or free text.
