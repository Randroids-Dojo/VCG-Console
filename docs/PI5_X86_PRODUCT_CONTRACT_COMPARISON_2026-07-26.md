# Raspberry Pi 5 and ordinary x86 identical-product-contract comparison

Date: 2026-07-26
Scope: I-173
Status: blocked strict zero-result pre-registration

## Claim boundary

This campaign defines the evidence required to compare the required Raspberry
Pi 5 plus AI HAT+ 26 TOPS lower-cost reference against the required ordinary
x86-64 Linux premium reference under one identical product contract. It
records no received hardware, build, install, camera exposure, participant,
gameplay session, measurement, recovery attempt, cost result, qualification,
or reference-tier selection.

Existing development-host behavior, vendor specifications, quotes, component
plans, package tests, camera or controller campaigns, synthetic benchmarks,
or results from a different target do not establish integrated product
behavior on either required target. Each required target must independently
pass every applicable cell. A component result, aggregate, other target, or
optional Steam Machine row cannot rescue a required-target failure.

The plan does not authorize purchase, assembly, installation, camera or
controller operation, participant collection, hosted-service mutation, fault
injection, power interruption, destructive recovery, qualification,
publication, or a reference-tier change.

## Required and optional target rows

The comparison has two required rows:

1. `pi5-ai-hat26`: an aarch64 Raspberry Pi 5 with Hailo-8 AI HAT+ 26 TOPS,
   evaluated as the lower-cost reference; and
2. `ordinary-x86-linux`: a conventional x86-64 Linux PC with one qualified
   platform pose backend, evaluated as the premium reference.

`steam-machine-optional-later` is a third, separate compatibility row. It is
not required for I-173 and cannot redefine the common contract, qualify either
required target, rescue a failure, or change a reference tier. Its evidence
must remain independently labelled and separately reviewed.

Before operation, bind the exact hardware, firmware, operating-system image,
kernel, drivers, runtime, model, RAM, storage, cooling, power, display, camera,
controller, room, network, and instrument tuple for each required target.
Changing a material tuple invalidates the affected result and requires the
applicable cells to be rerun under the frozen rule.

## Identical product contract

Both required targets use Motion API `0.4.0` and the fixed
`body.core17`, `actions.obstacle.v1`, and `actions.shell.v1` profiles. They
must use the same source, game manifests, schemas, actions, visible UX,
acceptance gates, shared wide-angle UVC camera contract, standards-conformant
controller mapping, reserved-action ownership, accountless launcher, tracker,
profile, signed-package, and retro-game behavior.

Architecture, accelerator, driver, packaging, and model-runtime differences
are permitted only behind reviewed interfaces. They may not create a reduced
game catalog, altered Motion semantics, easier action definitions, downgraded
visual behavior, different offline scope, weaker update or recovery rules, or
target-specific acceptance gates. A desk plan or isolated component pass does
not substitute for integrated target evidence.

## Workloads

The same seven workload identities and interactions apply to each required
target:

1. the launcher shell, including reserved actions and offline navigation;
2. the local Obstacle Motion sample;
3. one selected signed local package with a manifest-declared profile;
4. offline Retro 2048 through the libretro path;
5. VibeBots compatibility;
6. Mi Casa Es Su Casa compatibility; and
7. Determined compatibility.

The first four paths are offline-required. The three hosted compatibility
titles are network-required and receive no title Motion frames. Their load or
heartbeat cannot establish gameplay, accountless core operation, Motion
integration, controller support, focus correctness, or recovery. Exact
content, versions, interactions, service/account boundaries, cleanup, and
non-destructive mutation rules remain blocked pending owner review.

Seventeen scenarios across two required targets form 34 required cells. Each
cell requires 20 valid cycles, for 680 valid cycles total. The optional Steam

## Fixed acceptance boundary

Each required target/scenario cell needs 20 valid cycles. Concurrent workload
cells include at least 3,600 measured seconds. Fixed ceilings are 120 ms for
exposure-to-action p95, 60 seconds for cold boot to launcher readiness, five
seconds for warm resume, 15 seconds for local launch, 30 seconds for hosted
launch, 35 dBA at one metre, and 65,000 cents for the delivered Pi reference
build.

The campaign permits zero:

- Steam-account dependencies for core local operation;
- privileged false activations;
- reserved actions delivered to games or swallowed;
- successful microphone track, buffer, sample, or returned-byte access;
- untrusted camera access or retained raw media;
- undeclared root writes, network, IPC, filesystem, or device access;
- credential, account, profile/save, participant, stable-identifier, path, or
  free-text disclosures;
- unrecovered update, power-cut, fault, or rebuild failures; and
- valid required-target product failures.

Every required-target cell must pass. The optional row and aggregates cannot
rescue failure, and all open gates must be frozen before operation.

## Open acceptance boundary

Twenty-two outcome-sensitive gates remain null: per-action precision and
recall; pose and game FPS; game p95 frame time; capture and pose drop rates;
exposure timestamp uncertainty; per-target CPU, GPU or accelerator use, RAM,
storage growth, wall power, idle energy, temperature, and throttle events;
per-scenario fault recovery; update rollback; rebuild and reinstall time;
ordinary-x86 reuse and replacement delivered cost; and minimum cost difference
between the required references.

Freeze exact thresholds, units, sampling, percentile treatment, uncertainty,
invalidity, per-cell application, and independent review before the first
affected operation. Results must not be used to tune a gate retroactively.
