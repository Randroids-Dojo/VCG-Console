## VRB-001: exact targets and campaign staging

Which qualified camera, mode, capture policy, lens calibration, room geometry
and target configurations enter I-041, and does one target run first as a
non-qualifying protocol rehearsal?

Safe default: bind every exact configuration after its prerequisite campaign
passes. A rehearsal cannot qualify another target or become a result by rename.

## VRB-002: skin-tone measurement and stratum definitions

Which consented, reviewed method defines the five opaque skin-tone sampling
strata under measured lighting, and what exact ranges, repeatability and
assignment-disagreement rules apply?

Safe default: keep ranges and assignment null. Do not infer race, ethnicity,
identity, health or profile state, and do not ask the tracker to assign strata.

## VRB-003: cohort size and recruitment

What minimum distinct participant count per persona/stratum supports the
intended claim, how are repeat participants handled, and what recruitment and
compensation review is required for adults and school-age children?

Safe default: make no population or fairness claim from the one-person
prototype minimum. Freeze cohort and analysis power before recruitment; one
person cannot populate multiple strata.

## VRB-004: garment identity and measurement

Which exact solid, fine-pattern, large-pattern, patterned lower-body, loose-
sleeve, loose-leg and layered garments enter the campaign, and how are pattern
contrast/spatial frequency, fit, dimensions, reflectance and occluded body

Safe default: use a frozen received-item manifest with clean ordinary garments
in appropriate participant-selected sizes. Similar-looking substitutions and
post-result item swaps are prohibited.

## VRB-005: loose-garment movement safety

Which garment clearances, footwear/floor checks, movement limits, supervision,
stop rules and exclusion conditions make jump, duck and dodge trials safe?

Safe default: no full-motion loose-garment trial until an active-play reviewer
approves it. Stop on trip, snag, entanglement, heat, balance or participant
concern; consent cannot waive a failed condition.

## VRB-006: bounded blanket probes

Which exact blanket sizes, weights, materials, drape locations and assistant or
support methods define the three stationary probes?

Safe default: neutral and slow arms-raised only. No jump, duck, dodge, rapid
turn, head/face/neck/airway covering, floor drag, wrapping, tying, pinning or
restraint.

## VRB-007: clutter and moving-display fixtures

Which measured static textures, household-object boundaries and moving
television content define clutter without adding unsafe objects, private media,
unlicensed material or a hidden second-person interference test?

Safe default: use rights-cleared nonidentifying fixtures outside the play zone,
record occupancy/edge density/display motion, and keep people/mirrors/pets/
passersby in their separately governed interference campaign.

## VRB-008: lighting interaction policy

Which I-040 lighting strata are blocking for I-041, and must every appearance
condition cross every qualified lighting state or only a precommitted worst-
case subset?

Safe default: do not infer cross-lighting robustness. Freeze a source-bound
interaction rule before results and report any untested combination as absent.

## VRB-009: pose and action gates

What minimum core-17 detection, maximum missing/error/jitter, dropout and
reacquisition gates apply per persona/stratum/condition/placement/action cell?

Safe default: retain 95% precision, 90% recall, zero unintended privileged
actions and 120 ms exposure-to-game p95, but keep new gates null until the
ground-truth uncertainty and gameplay consequences are approved.

## VRB-010: worst-stratum disparity rule

What maximum performance gap is acceptable across skin-tone strata, which
metric and confidence interval define it, and what happens when every stratum
misses an absolute gate equally?

Safe default: require both absolute per-cell gates and a frozen disparity gate;
neither replaces the other. Publish no fairness claim when cohort or uncertainty
is inadequate.

## VRB-011: ground truth and appearance instrumentation

Which independent pose/action oracle, exposure clock, illuminance/colorimeter,
pattern/contrast method, clutter measurement, operators, calibration and
uncertainty budgets apply?

Safe default: bind them before collection. Candidate confidence and tracker-
derived appearance labels cannot provide ground truth.

## VRB-012: temporary images, sensitive data and deletion

May temporary diagnostic images be used, who may access them, how long may they
exist, how is deletion verified, and what minimum-cohort/reidentification rule
governs opaque-stratum aggregate release?

Safe default: no temporary images. Release no individual-level skin-tone,
garment or appearance data, stable identifiers, raw frames/video or free text;
allow only reviewed bounded aggregates.

## VRB-013: participants, operators and schedule authority

Who may consent for adults, consent and assent for children, operate the camera
and safety stop, prepare garments/fixtures, score ground truth, and approve the

Safe default: no physical session. Separate safety supervision from scoring,
retain every invalid/stopped cell, honor immediate withdrawal and do not
replace a failed participant, stratum or fixture after results are visible.
