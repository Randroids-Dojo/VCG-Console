ground truth for floor geometry, active play-zone boundaries, player-relative
scale, neutral stance, and usable movement range?

Why this is still an owner question: the current fixture repeats authored
confidence facts. An estimator cannot qualify itself by reproducing its own
output, and a visually plausible overlay is not bounded geometric or safety
evidence. The choice affects equipment, consent, room setup, retained
artifacts, uncertainty, and the later Q-205 threshold decision.

- do not buy equipment or begin a participant/camera campaign;
- do not treat synthetic exact repeatability as physical repeatability;

- pre-register exact camera identity/mode/pose, room and zone measurements,
  reference procedure, cohort cells, repetitions, failure handling, and
