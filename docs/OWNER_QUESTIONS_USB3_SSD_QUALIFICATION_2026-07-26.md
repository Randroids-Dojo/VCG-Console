# Owner Questions: USB 3 SSD Qualification

These questions block an execution-ready I-021 plan, purchase, destructive
operation, qualification, selection, and product-BOM change. They do not block
desk validation of the zero-result plan. Existing Q-199 through Q-202 remain
open and authoritative.

## SSD-001: valid fallback trigger

Which canonical rejected microSD result, failure code, independent root-cause
review, and architecture-applicability evidence may invoke the production SSD
campaign?

Safe default: accept only the tracked microSD plan/result pair with conclusion
`rejected`, complete valid/adverse evidence, and a reviewed explanation that an
SSD could address the attributable storage failure. Harness, software, target,
operator, or unexplained failures do not invoke production I-021.

## SSD-002: candidate and sample authority

May a lab-only sample be acquired before a valid microSD rejection, and which
exact current integrated SSD, seller, return terms, maximum delivered price,
received-unit count, and manufacturing-lot scope are permitted?

Safe default: no purchase. If separately authorized, label the unit lab-only;
it cannot qualify the product, mutate D-047/D-048/D-111, or substitute for a
fresh production candidate and delivered quote.

## SSD-003: capacity and layout

May the smallest practical integrated SSD exceed 256GB, and if so must unused
capacity remain unallocated or may it join the bounded writable partition?

Safe default: preserve the qualified logical layout, quotas, bounded caches and
logs, write projection, recovery reserve, and supported content promise. Extra
capacity supplies no policy, endurance, or product-scope credit.

## SSD-004: service and performance gates

What service horizon, host-write projection, endurance margin, allowable
performance drift, cold/warm boot p95, per-operation storage p95, update time,
unit/lot count, stop conditions, family-rejection rule, and retest policy apply?

Safe default: freeze every value before receipt or execution evidence is
visible; require every exact unit and every mandatory phase to pass, with no
aggregate or faster-throughput rescue.

## SSD-005: USB, UAS/BOT, and power

What exact Pi/EEPROM/kernel/firmware/power/USB topology and instrumentation
freeze the 1.6A shared USB and complete 27W system margin, and can BOT ever be a
reviewed production fallback after UAS faults?

Safe default: require stable bootloader and production-kernel enumeration,
UAS operation, measured inrush/peak/steady margin with the complete assembly,
and zero unsafe current overrides. Do not silently downgrade to BOT or add a
powered hub/separate supply.

## SSD-006: destructive schedule

How many valid scheduled cuts, live disconnects, intermittent-contact trials,
corruption injections, update interruptions, and blank-device recoveries are
required for every authoritative state, unit, lot, and writer platform?

Safe default: use a counterbalanced pre-registered schedule with independent
cut/contact/fault oracles; retain all scheduled, invalid, stopped, retried,
adverse, and worst cases. Zero committed corruption remains non-negotiable.

## SSD-007: mechanical and thermal integration

Which internal bracket, fasteners, insulation, cable routing, bend radius,
retention force, connector-cycle count, handling/drop/pull cases, airflow,
temperature limit, EMI/coexistence cases, service tools, and replacement policy
qualify the physical assembly?

Safe default: require an enclosure-integrated serviceable nonconductive mount,
positive retention, strain relief, thermal clearance, and no adhesive-only or
externally tethered reference design.

## SSD-008: recovery release and target selection

Which signed recovery release, supported Windows/macOS/Linux writers, exact
target-selection oracle, destructive confirmation UX, write/read-back method,
offline first-boot cases, wrong/old/cloned-device behavior, and permanent local
data-loss disclosure apply?

Safe default: no recovery mutation until the tool proves the exact intended
device independently, verifies the whole image, boots offline, fails closed on
ambiguity, and never claims to restore profiles or saves after media loss.

## SSD-009: delivered cost and product comparison

What quote jurisdiction, currency, seller class, freshness window, shipping,
tax, mount, cable, service hardware, return/warranty, replacement, volume, and
mass evidence must stay within D-111, and when does failure require an x86 or
new hardware-tier comparison?

Safe default: require the complete delivered lower-cost reference build to
remain at or below $650. A lab discount, used/open-box price, missing mount,
unpriced shipping/tax, or spare-equipment accounting cannot qualify the
production BOM.

## SSD-010: authority, evidence, and review

Who approves purchase/return, firmware mutation, imaging, destructive writes,
power cuts, disconnects, corruption, recovery-media writes, target operation,
the frozen schedule/gates, sanitized result schema, retention/deletion,
incident stop rules, independent review, qualification, publication, and BOM
mutation?

Safe default: authorize none of them from the tracked blocked plan. Require
separate exact digests and explicit authority before operation; keep result
`null` until then.
