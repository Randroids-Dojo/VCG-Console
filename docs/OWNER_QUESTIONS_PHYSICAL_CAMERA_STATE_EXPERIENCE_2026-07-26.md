# Owner questions: physical shutter and camera-state experience

Date: 2026-07-26

Safe defaults authorize no camera or enclosure selection, purchase, device
operation, shutter cycling, indicator or idle/suspend test, participant
collection, product-surface change, result publication, qualification, or
product claim.

## CS-001: exact received camera and enclosure inventory

Which exact camera, enclosure, revision, firmware, cable, mount, power path,
shutter mechanism, indicator hardware, and condition represent each of the
ordinary x86, SteamOS, and Pi 5 target-package roles?

Safe default: all three remain blocking. Merchandise pages, another target,
an opened or modified unit, and a visually similar revision cannot substitute.

## CS-002: physical shutter construction and independent truth

What mechanism and lens area does the shutter cover, how are open, partial,
jammed, and closed positions independently observed, and what optical fixture,
instrument, uncertainty, leakage limit, force limit, wear rule, and quarantine
procedure apply?

Safe default: software remains `NOT SENSED`. Never infer shutter position from
black frames, low landmarks, tracker state, or the activity indicator.

## CS-003: hardware activity-indicator authority

Is the indicator electrically tied to sensor exposure, USB streaming, device
power, firmware intent, host intent, or application state? Freeze its colors,
patterns, luminance, field, latency, failure behavior, and independent probe.

Safe default: a power-only or application-intent lamp cannot qualify as a
capture activity indicator. No capture may occur without truthful indication.

## CS-004: complete capture-path and native-owner inventory

Which component owns camera leases, and which motion, calibration, profile,
diagnostic, update, recovery, hosted, or developer paths can open a camera?
How are requested, granted, stream-active, frame-arriving, tracker-ready, and
optically usable facts reconciled?

Safe default: require one closed native authority and an immutable exhaustive
release inventory. Unknown or contradictory state stops capture.

## CS-005: product surface location

Must camera state remain visible in Motion Lab, setup/settings, ordinary play,
pause/recovery, or a persistent console-owned header? Which of the three
registered surface roles are blocking, and may another candidate be added?

Safe default: test all three registered roles without cross-surface rescue.
A hosted game may not replace or contradict console-owned camera state.

## CS-006: stop, idle, suspend, crash, and resume policy

Freeze idle duration, capture-release deadline, indicator-off deadline,
suspend entry and resume behavior, disconnect/reconnect behavior, process-crash
cleanup, retry limits, user confirmation, and escalation or quarantine rules.

Safe default: stop fail-closed and never auto-resume capture after idle,
suspend, disconnect, or crash. Require an explicit fresh restart.

## CS-007: room, display, lighting, distance, and angle protocol

Select the television/output mode, camera/enclosure placements, play zone,
seating and approach points, 2.5 m and 4.5 m viewing roles, 0/30/60-degree
angles, daylight/warm-lamp/TV-only/dim ranges, instruments, and repeat rules.

Safe default: all registered conditions remain blocking; browser viewport
screenshots and one bright head-on view cannot qualify the room.

## CS-008: personas, accessibility, consent, and safety

Who may approve and recruit the five persona classes? Freeze adult consent,
child assent and guardian consent, accessibility accommodations, mobility and
vision protocol, rest, stop, compensation, privacy, retention, deletion,
adverse-event handling, and independent ground truth.

Safe default: camera-free rehearsal first, then an authorized adult-only dry
run. No child or accessibility claim before its exact reviewed protocol.

## CS-009: optical, indicator, and state-transition gates

Freeze pre-result attenuation, leakage, indicator luminance/contrast/viewing
field, shutter force/wear, capture-release, indicator-off, state-detection,
stale-state, and recovery limits with instrument uncertainty and cell-level
disposition rules.

Safe default: every open gate remains null. Results cannot author thresholds,
and later recovery cannot erase a contradiction or unsafe event.

## CS-010: comprehension and reach gates

Freeze exact uncoached prompts and limits for software-access identification,
stream-activity identification, correct `NOT SENSED` shutter understanding,
recovery selection, indicator detection, shutter reach, connector/service
reach, task time, force, errors, discomfort, and unsafe-zone entry.

Safe default: all five personas and every surface, state, distance, angle,
lighting, package, task, and approach cell must independently pass.

## CS-011: evidence privacy and diagnostics

May any temporary room image, optical frame, audio, accessibility note,
participant identifier, exact age, serial, path, provider message, or free text
be collected? Who controls storage, access, retention, deletion, and incidents?

Safe default: release only path-free closed-vocabulary aggregates. Authorize
temporary diagnostics separately and verify deletion; collect no audio.

## CS-012: execution and decision authority

Who may authorize purchase, device power, shutter cycling, indicator tests,
idle/suspend/fault injection, adult or child trials, raw diagnostics, product
surface implementation, result publication, qualification, or claims?

Safe default: nobody under this plan. Resolve each authority explicitly before
the corresponding action.
