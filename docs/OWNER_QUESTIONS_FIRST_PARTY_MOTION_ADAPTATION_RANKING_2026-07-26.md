# Owner questions: first-party motion-adaptation ranking

Date: 2026-07-26

Status: sixteen unresolved choices; none authorizes scoring, ranking or adaptation

Safe defaults keep I-103 blocked, preserve the obstacle-only first milestone and
prevent a weighted score from overriding product gates.

## FPMR-001: obstacle milestone prerequisite

Which exact obstacle-sample result closes the first Motion API milestone before
existing-game ranking can begin?

Safe default: require every frozen one-player action, latency, controller,
loading, recovery and target gate to pass. A desk demo, synthetic trace or
partial result does not open I-103.

## FPMR-002: one-player prerequisite

Which exact first-real-room one-player result, target, camera, room, personas,
placements, lighting and action contract becomes the ranking baseline?

Safe default: use the complete qualified I-053/I-210 result only. Do not infer
physical comfort, comprehension, safety or accuracy from synthetic evidence.

## FPMR-003: two-player prerequisite

Which exact I-054 result proves identity continuity, independent action,
calibration, interference, freeze, menu ownership, join/leave and recovery?

Safe default: require the entire two-player matrix to pass. One-player success,
a two-person demo, aggregate scores or four-player aspirations cannot substitute.

## FPMR-004: shell-navigation prerequisite

Which exact controller-only shell, launch, loading, Back, retry, return and
shutdown result establishes stable platform behavior before game ranking?

Safe default: require the complete cold-start usability result on both required
tiers with no keyboard, mouse, hidden setup or guesswork.

## FPMR-005: exact candidate pool freeze

Does the ranking use the current 23-game first-party rights-screen pool, or will
an owner-approved later catalog snapshot replace it?

Safe default: retain all 23 current IDs. A pool change creates a new plan and
reruns every cell; no candidate is dropped for inconvenience or preference.

## FPMR-006: ownership and rights eligibility

What exact code, asset, font, audio, model, title/trademark, contributor,
service, deployment, corresponding-source, notice, change-authority and
maintenance-owner closure makes a candidate eligible?

Safe default: require complete accountable closure for every shipped or hosted
byte. Public source, organization membership and package metadata grant no
authority.

## FPMR-007: mechanic-fit evidence

What artifact defines each candidate's current mechanic/state/input map and one
bounded proposed motion concept, including controller equivalence, cancellation,
failure and safety semantics?

Safe default: one closed source-bound concept packet per title. Do not build a
prototype or score from a genre, screenshot, imagined gesture or marketing copy.

## FPMR-008: accessibility eligibility

Which standing, seated, partial-motion, fatigue, reach, contraindication,
controller-equivalence, comprehension and recovery checks are blocking?

Safe default: both blocking personas and every approved route must pass. No
silent body substitution or score normalization; a controller alternative must
preserve the game goal and recovery path.

## FPMR-009: latency and target evidence

What pre-adaptation evidence may establish game-integration timing headroom on
ordinary x86-64 Linux and Raspberry Pi, and when is a bounded technical spike
necessary?

Safe default: use exposure-authoritative instrumentation that includes the game
boundary and concurrent workload. Keep 120 ms p95 fixed. If a spike is needed,
authorize it separately after all earlier prerequisites and rights boundaries.

## FPMR-010: multiplayer-value evidence

Which one-/two-player tasks demonstrate actual adaptation value rather than
nominal multiplayer support?

Safe default: require identity, per-player action, simultaneous-action,
interference, shared-state, freeze, menu, join/leave, loss and recovery evidence.
Do not award value for network or unqualified four-player modes.

## FPMR-011: code-effort and maintenance protocol

How should implementation, testing, packaging, migration, service, update and
annual maintenance effort be estimated and independently reviewed?

Safe default: source-bound work-breakdown ranges with explicit uncertainty,
dependencies and accountable owners. Repository size, language and one-number
person-hour guesses are not scores.

## FPMR-012: showcase-value protocol

Which household tasks, comprehension questions, failure disclosures, repeat-use
measure and novelty-decay interval establish truthful showcase value?

Safe default: pre-register tasks across both blocking personas and retain every
adverse result. Popularity, visual novelty and reviewer enthusiasm do not count.

## FPMR-013: weights, eligibility thresholds and tie breaks

What ppm weight and minimum eligible score applies to each of the seven
dimensions, what constitutes a material lead, how many candidates may be
ranked, and what exact tie-break sequence applies?

Safe default: leave every value null until approved together. Weights must sum
to 1,000,000 ppm and may never override a separate blocking gate.

## FPMR-014: independent reviewers and adjudication

Who is qualified to review each dimension, what conflicts disqualify a reviewer,
how is blind scoring enforced, and how are disagreements adjudicated?

Safe default: two independent reviews per cell before either reviewer sees an
aggregate. Preserve both originals, conflict records and the closed adjudicated
disposition.

## FPMR-015: uncertainty, sensitivity and evidence expiry

What normalization, missing-data, uncertainty, sensitivity, evidence cutoff,
expiry, regression and reranking rules apply?

Safe default: no imputation. Publish complete sensitivity across approved
weight bounds, expire any candidate whose source/build/deployment/service or
product gate changes, and rerun the full affected matrix.

## FPMR-016: ranking, selection and adaptation authority

Who may authorize evidence gathering, scoring, ranking, owner selection, source
or service access, a bounded adaptation scope/budget, rights/security/
accessibility/release review, packaging, admission and publication?

Safe default: treat these as separate approvals. A completed ranking selects
nothing, creates no first-milestone work and grants no adaptation or product
authority.
