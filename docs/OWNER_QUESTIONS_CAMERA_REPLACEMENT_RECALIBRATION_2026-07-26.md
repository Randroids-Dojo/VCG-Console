## CRR-001: exact target and service fixtures

camera, mount, cable, connector, enclosure, fastener, power and tool tuples
define the blocking fixtures?

Safe default: require distinct exact digests for every target. A PC fixture
does not qualify Steam or the integrated appliance.

## CRR-002: old and replacement device identities

unit, an approved substitute, an unapproved revision and a wrong camera? Which

VID/PID, product text, port order, image appearance and profile names are
insufficient alone.

## CRR-003: persisted identity implementation

Which protected local store, schema, generation, transaction, recovery and
anti-rollback rules persist the current camera binding? Who owns mutation?
