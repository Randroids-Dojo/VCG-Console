## Q-238: supported actions, alternates, and qualification gates

Which seated, partial-body, limited-range, and helper-present cases are in the
first supported scope; which exact body or controller alternatives may each
case use; and what per-case participant count, false-action, missed-action,
latency, comfort/fatigue, cancellation, recovery, and comprehension gates must
pass before support is claimed?

Safe default: advertise none of these body-play modes yet. Keep seated
gameplay and helper-overlap body paths blocked, preserve action-specific
missing-landmark suppression, offer only the existing deliberately assigned
controller path, and report no-controller cases as unsupported. Never remap
Home, Back, or Pause; never infer helper control; never extrapolate landmarks;
and never normalize scores or thresholds silently. Before collection, require
accessibility/safety review, participant-authored comfortable movement
envelopes and stop rules, separate cohort reporting, both target tiers, and
owner-selected per-action gates that cannot be hidden by aggregate results.
