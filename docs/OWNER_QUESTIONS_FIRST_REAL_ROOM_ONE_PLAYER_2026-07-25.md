# Owner questions: first real-room one-player campaign — 2026-07-25

The strict campaign remains blocked without these decisions. None of the safe
defaults grants room access, participant recruitment, photography, camera
collection, purchase or product authority.

## QRR-001: first target and clean build

Which exact target configuration should host the first session, and which
clean-build artifact identifies the software under test?

Safe default: use no target by assumption. Treat the Windows workstation and
present C920 as development inventory only; require an exact hardware, OS,
driver, runtime, tracker/model and clean-build digest before collection.

## QRR-002: selected room, play zone and five placements

Which completed room survey establishes the primary room and safe 8 by 8 ft
zone, and what exact measured coordinates define center plus the four required
zone-edge placements?

Safe default: wait for the strict room-survey result. Do not infer edges from a
camera preview, carpet pattern, estimated stride or empty-room arrangement.

## QRR-003: camera mode, capture policy and lighting state

Which qualified camera/mode/placement and selected I-036 capture policy should
the run use, and which measured I-040 lighting condition is blocking?

Safe default: require one exact camera tuple and a pre-result capture-policy
selection. Do not treat advertised 1080p60, automatic controls or the current
C920 fixture as qualified.

## QRR-004: adult and child participation authority

Who may approve the adult session and the school-age-child session, and what
consent, assent, comprehension, stop and withdrawal protocol applies?

Safe default: schedule neither. Require adult consent plus age-appropriate
child assent, an independent stop path, no coercive retest, and separate
persona results. The persona specification alone is not participation
authority.

## QRR-005: pose stability gates

What minimum pose FPS and core-17 coverage, and what maximum torso-normalized
jitter, must every persona/placement cell meet?

Safe default: keep all three gates null and block collection. Select them from
product needs and independent prior evidence, not after seeing this result.

## QRR-006: independent ground truth and exposure clock

Which independent action/transition labeling method and trustworthy camera
exposure clock will bind each run? Who operates and reviews them?

Safe default: require separately SHA-256-bound protocols and reviewers. Camera
output cannot label itself, capture arrival cannot become exposure time, and
inference or display timing cannot qualify D-110.

## QRR-007: negative-session script and privileged-action oracle

Which fixed 15-minute script covers ordinary play, idle body motion,
spectators/passersby, television imagery, reflective surfaces where present,
partial occlusion and controller use? Which independent oracle identifies
unintended Back, Pause, Home, Resume and Exit?

Safe default: pre-register identical ordered categories for every cell and
retain every emitted privileged action. Do not shorten a failed run or replace
it with another condition.

## QRR-008: minimized data and temporary diagnostics

Is skeleton-only evidence sufficient for all scoring and reproduction? If a
temporary diagnostic image is necessary, who authorizes its purpose, access,
retention deadline and verified deletion?

Safe default: skeleton-only, no raw recording, no raw frames in repository or
release, no names/stable identifiers and no face/body-profile templates. Keep
temporary diagnostic images disabled until a separate approved protocol exists.

## QRR-009: operators, instruments and schedule validity

Which operators, calibrated lux/optical/timing instruments, session order,
breaks, maximum daily volume, invalid-attempt reasons and stop rules apply?

Safe default: bind all of them before collection, randomize or counterbalance
only through a reviewed schedule, retain invalid attempts in place, and stop
for safety, consent withdrawal or invalid ground truth without silently
replacing data.
