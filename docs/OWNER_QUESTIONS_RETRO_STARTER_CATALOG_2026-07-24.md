# Owner questions: retro starter catalog

Date: 2026-07-24

The first source-backed screen approved zero of six candidates. These choices
determine which candidate should receive expensive artifact and target work.

## SC-001: noncommercial titles

May a public VCG DIY release bundle a title whose code or assets forbid
commercial use, such as Dinothawr?

Safe default: no. Prefer terms that permit downstream source/binary
redistribution and ordinary commercial use; do not make every DIY redistributor
interpret whether cost recovery, prebuilt media, video, events, or support is
commercial.

## SC-002: first deep-review candidate

Should the next complete asset/trademark audit target Mr.Boom, seek explicit
licenses for Lutro Snake, replace 2048’s embedded font, or create a small
first-party libretro fixture/title?

Safe default: ask the Lutro demo authors for explicit licensing while
independently reviewing Mr.Boom’s embedded assets; keep a first-party fixture
as the schedule-safe qualification fallback.

## SC-003: starter catalog size

How many independently maintained titles are necessary for the first public
image?

Safe default: one fully qualified title is better than several ambiguous
titles; expand only after the complete admission/package/update/removal path is
repeatable.

## SC-004: title and trademark review

Who reviews names, screenshots, character/art similarity, sound/music, and
store/catalog wording for clone-derived projects such as Mr.Boom?

Safe default: require a documented legal/title review separate from the source
license, and use no third-party franchise logo or implied affiliation.

## SC-005: content and age notes

Which content taxonomy and family-mode reviewer records violence, flashing,
difficulty, multiplayer interaction, language, and accessibility notes?

Safe default: publish a conservative title-specific note even for simple
homebrew; absence of a commercial rating is not an “everyone” rating.

## SC-006: update and removal owner

Who pins upstream revisions, reviews new assets/dependencies, rebuilds both
architectures, responds to security/license changes, and owns emergency
removal?

Safe default: one named repository role owns every admitted title, with a
frozen last-known-good package and a tested removal path that preserves saves.

## SC-007: developer samples

Should the MIT libretro samples be packaged in developer images as hidden
input/audio/video qualification fixtures?

Safe default: yes only in an explicitly non-catalog developer namespace after
exact build closure and target qualification; never count them as starter
games or expose them in family mode.
