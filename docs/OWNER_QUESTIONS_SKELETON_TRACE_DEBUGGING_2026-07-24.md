# Owner questions: skeleton-trace debugging

Date: 2026-07-24

The bounded v2 schema and synthetic exercise are complete without these
answers. The questions gate real-player collection, support retention, and
any escalation beyond portable core17 traces. No answer is assumed.

## Q-237: retention, blind reviewer, and escalation authority

Please select the production direction for each of these boundaries.

### 1. Exported-trace retention

How long may a deliberately exported skeleton trace remain on the console or
support workstation?

Please specify:

- maximum age;
- maximum total bytes and file count;
- whether expiry is automatic or user-confirmed;
- whether files may enter a managed support queue;
- whether export is available only in local-admin mode; and
- what confirmation proves deletion from console, removable media, support
  workstation, backups, and tickets.

### 2. Independent blind exercise

May I coordinate a consented real-camera blind debugging exercise with a
reviewer who did not author the trace, bug truth, or analyzer?

If yes, please identify or authorize:

- reviewer role;
- participant/household consent and child-assent boundary;
- approved defect classes;
- session count;
- whether the reviewer may receive stable hardware/backend/configuration
  identifiers; and
- where the minimized artifacts may be stored during review.

### 3. Defect-specific escalation

Which additional minimized artifacts may be requested after a v2 trace is
formally scored insufficient?

Please approve or reject each separately:

- qualified camera-exposure timestamps;
- calibrated floor transform plus independent contact labels;
- bounded detector-candidate/association decisions with trace-local IDs;
- core17 rule/smoothing/threshold state transitions;
- MediaPipe 33-point or provider-world landmarks;
- closed target telemetry for USB, scheduling, power, memory, and thermals;
  and
- a separately consented short raw-video laboratory capture.

Approval of one item should not authorize the others or turn it into routine
collection.

## Safe default while unanswered

- Keep collection volatile and core17-only until a deliberate local export.
- Require at least one frame and emit only the bounded v2 schema.
- Never include raw frames, audio, portraits, profile IDs, free text, rich
  landmarks, world coordinates, or provider exception text.
- Do not upload, retain, or attach traces automatically.
- Treat the downloaded file as user-managed and instruct the user to delete it
  after the local debugging purpose ends.
- Do not run a real-player campaign or claim independent blind-debugging
  success.
- Stop at “symptom only” or “insufficient” when physical cause, exposure time,
  floor truth, or pre-association state is absent.
- Require a new explicit approval before any richer or raw artifact is
  collected.
