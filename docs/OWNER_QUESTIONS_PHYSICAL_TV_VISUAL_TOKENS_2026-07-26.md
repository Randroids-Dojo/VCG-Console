# Owner questions: physical-TV visual tokens

Date: 2026-07-26

These questions do not block validating the zero-result I-206 plan. They block
physical display access, participant work, camera capture, target execution,
results, accent selection, accessibility claims, and publication.

## I206-001: Exact build and complete surface states

Which release-candidate build and catalog projection should be frozen? Confirm
the exact real shell states for launcher Home, first-run setup, loading, manual
pause, tracking loss, profile portrait, retro library, recoverable errors, and
developer mode, including default, focused, fault, and recovery states.

## I206-002: Televisions, targets, seating, and lighting

Select the exact primary television, secondary panel, panel modes, ordinary
x86-64 Linux target, Raspberry Pi 5/Hailo target, output modes, seating
distances, room-light ranges, measurement tools, capture camera, tripod
geometry, and crop/redaction protocol. Headless Chrome or a desktop monitor
cannot substitute for these physical rows.

## I206-003: Shippable accent candidates

Should cyan alone be blocking while amber and violet remain exploratory, or
must all three candidates pass every cell before any multi-accent setting can
ship? Passing the campaign will not itself authorize an accent picker or decide
whether selection is console-wide, profile-local, or administrator-only.

## I206-004: Adult, child, and accessibility protocols

Approve participant recruitment, adult consent, child assent/guardian consent,
comprehension screening, stop authority, minimum cohort sizes, valid runs per
cell, compensation, accessibility accommodations, and independent scoring.

Define low-vision and color-vision observation strata without storing diagnoses
or inferring medical status. Decide which accessibility specialist reviews the
protocol and aggregate result.

## I206-005: Numeric readability and comprehension gates

Freeze minimum angular text height, focus luminance contrast, comprehension
rate, task error rate, and task-completion p95 before the first physical run.
Specify how critical copy, focus, fault meaning, Back/Home recovery, and
color-independent state recognition are scored.

## I206-006: GPU, animation, and comfort gates

Freeze p95 GPU frame time, sustained FPS, dropped-frame rate, GPU memory, wall
power, transition-timing error, and reduced-motion gates for both target tiers.
Select the clocks, power instrument, capture method, warm-up/cooldown, run
duration, invalid-run rules, and comfort/vestibular review protocol.

## I206-007: Evidence, privacy, and publication authority

Approve the path-free result schema, cropped person-free display captures,
temporary data access, retention/deletion proof, reviewer roles, schedule, and
publication threshold. Identify who may authorize television access,
participant sessions, camera capture, target execution, accessibility claims,
shipping-accent recommendations, and publication.
