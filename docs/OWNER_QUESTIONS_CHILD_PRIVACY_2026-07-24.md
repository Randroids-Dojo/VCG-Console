# Owner questions: child and family privacy

Date: 2026-07-24

These choices are required before qualified counsel can approve any networked
family beta. Safe defaults preserve the local prototype while keeping risky
features disabled.

## CP-001: operator and business model

Which legal entity/person operates VCG, and will the product be sold,
subscribed, supported commercially, donated, or run as a nonprofit/research
project?

Safe default: no public networked beta until the operator, contact, business
model, responsible privacy/security roles, and contracting authority are
recorded.

## CP-002: launch markets

Which countries and US states may receive, access, or receive support for the
beta?

Safe default: one counsel-reviewed market first. Geo-block or do not solicit
unsupported markets; do not infer that an online release is jurisdictionless.

## CP-003: age scope

What minimum age and intended age bands apply to the appliance, each Motion
game, and every hosted title?

Safe default: treat school-age children as intended users because the product
plan says so. Do not use a paper 13+ label to contradict design, marketing, or
reasonably likely household use.

## CP-004: parental role

Is the console always configured by an adult before a child uses it, and what
proves the person granting consent or changing network/privacy settings is a
parent/guardian?

Safe default: keep networked child data collection off. Body matching and a
shared local profile are not authentication; select a reviewed verification
method only if consent is legally required.

## CP-005: portraits and body matching

Should real profile portraits or persistent automatic body-profile matching
ship in the first networked beta?

Safe default: no. Use non-photographic art and manual profile choice; keep
calibration session-local until counsel approves the exact biometric-like
facts, consent, protection, deletion, and child/household behavior.

## CP-006: hosted games

Which hosted games are allowed for child profiles, and who is the operator/
controller for their account, IP/log, save, leaderboard, message, generation,
notification, analytics, and support data?

Safe default: none until each title has a reviewed data/service inventory,
contractual role, audience decision, notice/consent basis, retention,
security, and deletion path.

## CP-007: accounts and public identity

May a child create an account, display name, public leaderboard identity,
message, friend/contact link, or notification subscription?

Safe default: no account or public/social identity in the family beta. Keep
scores household-local and visibly unverified.

## CP-008: analytics, ads, and training

May any VCG or third-party service use child/family data for analytics,
advertising, personalization, engagement optimization, or model training?

Safe default: no. No ads, targeted advertising, third-party analytics,
cross-title profiling, engagement nudges, or training use.

## CP-009: notices and consent

Who writes and approves the adult notice, child-readable explanation, consent/
decline/withdraw flow, and material-change notice for each selected market?

Safe default: qualified counsel plus tested age-appropriate UX. Decline must
leave meaningful offline/local use and be as easy and visible as acceptance.

## CP-010: parent and child rights

Who responds to access, correction, deletion, withdrawal, appeal, complaint,
and incident requests, and how is authority verified without collecting more
data than necessary?

Safe default: do not launch until the workflow, identity verification,
deadlines, provider propagation, audit evidence, and support owner are tested.

## CP-011: school/research use

Will consoles be used in schools, camps, clinics, studies, or organized youth
programs?

Safe default: no institutional deployment under the household beta. Create a
separate counsel/ethics/consent/assent/data protocol before any such use.

## CP-012: data location and providers

Where will every hosted service process/store data, which subprocessors and
cross-border transfers exist, and what written assurances apply?

Safe default: local-only core plus no child-facing hosted service until the
complete provider/transfer register and agreements are approved.

## CP-013: health and accessibility claims

May VCG describe motion, breaks, calibration, body tracking, or accessibility
features as health, fitness, therapeutic, developmental, educational, or
safety benefits?

Safe default: no medical/therapeutic/developmental or guaranteed-safety claim.
Use narrow functional descriptions until evidence and qualified review support
more.
