# DIY distribution readiness campaign — 2026-07-26

Status: strict zero-result I-143/I-144 plan committed for review; execution and
release remain blocked

Authority: D-093, D-103, D-104, D-105, I-143, and I-144

## Purpose

VCG intends to publish an open-source DIY project, not sell or formally support
hardware. That scope narrows the product promise, but it is not evidence that
electrical, radio, optical-emission, thermal, consumer, privacy, accessibility,
license, or instruction obligations do not apply.

The canonical plan is
`benchmarks/diy-distribution-readiness/diy-distribution-readiness-plan-v1.json`.
It freezes what must be learned and reviewed after an exact release candidate,
operator, distribution path, and target markets exist. It does not provide
legal advice, select a market, approve a release, or claim certification.

## Existing engineering inputs

The plan binds eight current repository inputs:

- deterministic SBOM/license evidence and its two known release blockers;
- the household active-play safety checklist;
- the child and family privacy review brief;
- the data retention and deletion policy;
- the console operating-mode and authority boundary;
- falsifiable prototype success criteria;
- child/adult persona, accessibility, consent, and evidence boundaries; and
- the current curated third-party notices.

These are useful engineering sources. None is a qualified market-specific
applicability opinion, certification, home-pilot result, accessibility result,
or release approval. The validator rejects any attempt to promote them into
one.

## Frozen scope

The admitted release model is source code, build scripts, separately gated
software images/packages, a BOM, enclosure source/templates, and build/use/
recovery/safe-play instructions. The following remain outside the authorized
scope:

- hardware or kit sales;
- preassembled hardware or paid services;
- warranty-backed or formally supported hardware;
- certification claims;
- compatibility claims for arbitrary substitutions; and
- entitlement to third-party games or content.

Any move toward those excluded forms needs a superseding decision and a new
review. Calling the work DIY, open source, or no-warranty cannot waive an
applicable requirement.

## Applicability matrix

Every exact market, channel, and release candidate must receive separate
dispositions for five domains:

1. electrical;
2. radio;
3. IR, laser, and optical emissions;
4. thermal, fire, and materials; and
5. consumer-product requirements and instructions.

Each disposition must identify the exact candidate scope, authoritative source
identity/version/date, applicability analysis, required tests/records/labels/
instructions, responsible owner and independent qualified reviewer, adverse or
conflicting evidence, and dated result. `Not applicable` requires the same
market/candidate/source/reviewer evidence as an applicable disposition.

A component supplier statement does not qualify the assembled DIY reference.
Component certification does not automatically transfer to the enclosure,
cables, power chain, camera, radio layout, software instructions, or builder
substitutions. One domain cannot rescue another.

## Release-material review matrix

Eight material lanes are mandatory:

- safe-play, installation, and stop guidance;
- privacy, data-use, retention, deletion, and family notice;
- open-source licenses, attribution, notices, and source availability;
- no-warranty and no-formal-support scope;
- security reporting, known risk, and incident routing;
- assembly, operation, update, recovery, and disposal guidance;
- accessible/readable language and non-color cue review; and
- exact-hardware compatibility and substitution limitations.

Each lane receives four non-rescuing review layers: engineering/source review,
qualified legal/applicability review, home-pilot safety/comprehension review,
and target-TV accessibility/controller review. That yields 32 required review
cells. Missing, unavailable, adverse, and conflicted records stay visible;
post-result exclusion and best-case aggregation are forbidden.

## Fixed release gates

The future release result requires zero:

- unresolved applicable requirements;
- missing review cells;
- unsupported safety/privacy/accessibility/compliance claims;
- unresolved artifact license or redistribution blockers;
- unresolved critical/high risks;
- unsafe or misleading instructions;
- unreviewed child/family privacy blockers; and
- unresolved accessibility blocking failures.

Outcome-sensitive details remain deliberately null, including the exact
applicable requirement set, evidence standards, pilot/accessibility protocols,
cohorts, reviewer-conflict rules, candidate/source freeze windows, and signoff
format. They must be frozen before affected work begins, not inferred after a
favorable observation.

## Authority and data boundary

This plan authorizes no counsel/vendor/regulator contact, participant or
household recruitment, home evidence collection, purchase, hardware mutation,
electrical/radio/optical/thermal/destructive testing, filing, safety/privacy/
accessibility/warranty claim, publication, distribution, sale, or formal
support.

Future repository evidence is limited to authoritative public-source
identifiers/dates, exact artifact identities/hashes, closed review codes,
path-free aggregate counts, and coded adverse/conflict/blocker dispositions.
Names, addresses, precise locations, household media/layouts, raw camera or
biometric/health/accessibility payloads, secrets/payment details, device or
network identifiers, paths, support-bundle contents, correspondence, opinions,
and unbounded free text are prohibited.
