## LTV-002: dense-screen scrolling policy

Should dense settings and profile-management screens use a visible internal
scroll region, paged television panels, or a reduced set of primary choices?

Recommended provisional answer: prefer paged panels for ordinary family
surfaces and permit a visibly scrollable region only for advanced or
diagnostic content. Require focus movement to reveal the whole focused action
without moving the persistent Home/Back affordance.

## LTV-003: failure-dialog primary action

For offline, crashed, hung, and unavailable launches, should Retry or Exit be
the initial focus?

Recommended provisional answer: keep Exit initially focused until real
supervision can prove that Retry is safe, bounded, and likely to help.
Retain a clearly labeled Retry action where available and restore the exact
opener after dismissal.

## LTV-004: evidence retention

Should pixel-bound desk evidence retain every resolution/state PNG in Git, or
move older captures to a release artifact after the policy stabilizes?

Recommended provisional answer: keep the small candidate-contract and
representative-shell captures in Git while the layout is changing. Revisit
retention only after a signed release-evidence pipeline can preserve exact
hashes and durable access without weakening offline validation.
