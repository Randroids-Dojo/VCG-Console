# Owner questions: Hailo 13 TOPS and 26 TOPS comparison

Date: 2026-07-25

Investigation: I-158

The comparison plan is intentionally blocked and records zero results. Do not
buy hardware, collect participant data, retain replay frames or populate a
threshold from observed outcomes merely because this pre-registration exists.

## Q-258 - identical-input replay corpus authority

What exact corpus may provide the same ordered decoded input bytes to the
sequential 13 TOPS and 26 TOPS runs, and what consent, rights, protection,
retention, deletion-verification and derived-artifact rules apply?

Live performances on swapped HATs cannot be identical. A persistent replay is
therefore required for a literal same-input comparison, but it may contain
sensitive household or child imagery. To close Q-258, approve either a
rights-cleared public/synthetic corpus or a consented minimized local corpus;
freeze its byte-level digest, labels and decode contract; name access roles;
bound encrypted retention and deletion evidence; prohibit audio, identifiers
and free text; and state exactly which skeleton/aggregate artifacts may be
released. Q-253 still governs any temporary imagery used to create independent
labels.

Owners: privacy, legal, security, QA, participant/guardian.

## Q-259 - pre-result qualification and value thresholds

What exact per-cell pose, action, throughput, frame-drop, thermal, power and
minimum delivered-savings thresholds would justify reconsidering D-041's 26
TOPS baseline?

The plan already fixes the D-110 live exposure-to-action p95 ceiling at 120 ms,
zero privileged false activations, every-cell pass and no aggregate rescue. It
leaves the remaining thresholds null because no owner-approved rationale is
present. To close Q-259, freeze minimum detection, per-landmark p95 and worst
error, per-action precision/recall, pose/game FPS, drop ratio, sustained
temperature, throttle-event, wall-power and same-date delivered-savings gates;
define participant/placement/workload cells, sample sizes, uncertainty and
invalid-run policy; and record the decision before either variant's result is
visible.

Passing those gates permits an evidence review only. Replacing 26 TOPS still
requires a superseding decision; no score or script may select hardware
automatically.

Owners: product owner, ML, performance, QA, thermal, finance, accessibility,
safety.
