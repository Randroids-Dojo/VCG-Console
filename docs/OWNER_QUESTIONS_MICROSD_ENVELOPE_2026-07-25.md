# Owner questions: executable microSD qualification envelope

Last updated: 2026-07-25

The machine-checkable envelope adds no purchase or destructive-test authority.
The four questions already reserved in
`OWNER_QUESTIONS_MICROSD_QUALIFICATION_2026-07-24.md` remain the complete owner
boundary; this closeout records exactly how their answers populate a future
ready plan.

## Q-193: exact part and revision boundary

The ready plan needs one approved part number and either manufacturer-confirmed
alias evidence or a deliberately distinct test boundary for the quoted
`AN6IA` and manufacturer-listed `GN6IA` suffixes.

Conservative default while unanswered:

- approve neither suffix for qualification execution;
- treat substitutions, controller changes, and unexplained lot/revision drift
  as new boundaries;
- do not infer equivalence from capacity, packaging similarity, reseller text,
  or a successful read; and
- bind the eventual evidence and exact cohort before the first write.

## Q-194: destructive cohort, lot, and retained-control budget

The ready plan needs an exact tested-card minimum, independently purchased lot
minimum, retained-control requirement, purchase authorization, and destructive
test authorization.

Conservative default while unanswered:

- purchase no cards and destroy no media;
- retain the existing proposal of at least three tested cards across two lots
  plus one unpowered control as a proposal only;
- do not charge a lab cohort to the delivered reference BOM without an owner
  decision; and
- do not publish receipt/account data in the result.

## Q-195: service horizon, workload, and endurance margin

The ready plan needs projected high-case host writes per card, a margin ratio,
and frozen boot, storage-operation, capacity, and performance-drift gates from
the final representative workload.

Conservative default while unanswered:

- make no service-life or appliance-year claim;
- do not translate advertised Full HD recording hours into console writes;
- measure application payload and host/block submitted writes separately;
- keep every service-write, margin, capacity, deadline, and drift field null;
  and
- do not begin accelerated endurance before the final workload and image are
  reproducibly bound.

## Q-196: failure, retest, and USB SSD invocation

The ready plan and later conclusion need the approved root-cause/retest rule
and exact condition that invokes I-021 without pretending an arbitrary USB SSD
is qualified.

Conservative default while unanswered:

- never waive a valid failed cut, committed corruption, unverified launch,
  unauthorized reclamation, recovery failure, or unexplained media error;
- allow a proven harness defect to remain visible as invalid and never count
  it as a pass;
- require a new frozen plan and applicable fresh campaign after a software or
  layout change;
- reject aggregate rescue by another card or lot; and
- treat USB SSD as a new candidate requiring its own bridge, cable, power,
  disconnect, cost, enclosure, recovery, endurance, and fault evidence.

No prior question was answered, no new threshold was inferred, and no physical
operation was authorized by this tranche.
