# Owner Questions: Quote-date Hardware BOMs

These questions block a final delivered-cost result, not continued work on the
$0 owned-hardware prototype. They follow the profile-portrait tranche's
Q-179 through Q-183.

## Q-184: checkout jurisdiction and quote evidence

Which US state and ZIP code should the project use for purchase-time shipping
and tax quotes, and is the purchaser tax-exempt?

Please do not provide a street address. State and ZIP are sufficient for the
initial quote. If the actual purchaser or destination differs by seller, list
the applicable state/ZIP pair for each.

Current conservative default: do not open a destination-specific cart, guess a
tax rate, or claim the $650 delivered gate. The reference merchandise subtotal
is $576.23, leaving at most $73.77 for all seller shipping and tax.

Needed decision/evidence: quote jurisdiction, tax status, permission to retain
redacted cart screenshots, and whether the final evidence must use an
authorized purchaser's live checkout or a nonbinding reseller quote.

## Q-185: Pi and HAT revision acceptance

Which exact label should be accepted for the first Pi/HAT order when the
reseller identifies the Pi as `SC1112` but identifies the 26 TOPS AI HAT+ as
both `SC1791` and `SC1468` on the same page?

Current conservative default: accept neither ambiguity at checkout. Require
the reseller to identify the manufacturer part shipped, prohibit substitution,
and compare the received board/package labels with the quote before use.

Needed decision/evidence: approved reseller list, acceptable regional/packaging
aliases, authoritative Raspberry Pi mapping between the identifiers, permitted
silent board revisions, return response for a mismatch, and the hardware facts
that must be captured at receipt.

## Q-186: final camera cost/coverage tradeoff

Should the first complete reference quote continue using the finished Logitech
Brio `960-001105` at $169.99, or should the project first qualify a lower-cost
industrial wide-angle camera and its household enclosure/mount?

Current conservative default: use the Brio only as the financial placeholder
because it has a mature exact retail identity plus manufacturer-stated UVC,
1080p60, and 90° diagonal FOV. Do not order it until real C920 testing defines
the room gap and I-177 proves that 90° is wide enough. Do not replace it on
paper with the $69.80 ELP `ELP-USBFHD08S-H110`: that listing contradicts itself
on maximum resolution and does not include a qualified consumer enclosure,
mount, privacy shutter, indicator, or delivered US quote.

Needed decision/evidence: required horizontal/diagonal room coverage,
fixed-angle versus adjustable mount preference, physical privacy and indicator
requirements, whether camera enclosure/mount costs must fit within $650, and
whether lower rolling-shutter distortion is valuable enough to test an
industrial/global-shutter branch.

## Q-187: premium comparison authority

Should the premium BOM use the owner's actual Steam Machine preorder
configuration and invoice, or remain a reproducible public comparison against
Valve's 512GB base model without controller?

Current conservative default: retain both concepts separately. Use the public
$1,049 base price for a repeatable market comparison, but do not call
$1,225.44 a delivered owner BOM. Use the redacted invoice and receipt inventory
for the owner's actual unit after delivery.

Needed decision/evidence: ordered storage and controller bundle, item price,
discounts, shipping, tax, included HDMI/display cable, order status, and which
details may be retained in a tracked redacted artifact.
