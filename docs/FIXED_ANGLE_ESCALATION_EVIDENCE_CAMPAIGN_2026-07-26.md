## Purpose

D-092 keeps one fixed ultra-wide vertical camera axis as the lower-cost
appliance baseline. The adjustable prototype may discover that production
axis, but it is not a product feature. A manual tilt mechanism, dynamic
software auto-framing, or motorized aim may not be introduced merely because
one setup is inconvenient or because a mechanism looks attractive in a demo.

I-047 requires preserved evidence if representative-room testing cannot find a
fixed placement that passes. The tracked plan closes the method gap between a
failed fixed-placement campaign and any future owner decision. It does not
change D-092.

## Prerequisite failure evidence

Alternative evaluation remains blocked until a complete, source-bound
cross-tier camera-geometry result proves that zero fixed integrated pitches
qualify. The import must preserve:

- the exact received camera, enclosure, adjustable jig, target and room
  configuration;
- every geometric screen, blocking action and calibration cell;
- every failed, stopped, invalid and not-run observation;
- independent optical, floor and action ground truth; and
- a closed fixed-placement failure-cell ledger.

Null, incomplete, invalid or selectively retained evidence cannot declare the
fixed baseline failed. If one fixed pitch qualifies, the alternative campaign
is ineligible and D-092 remains the product direction.

## Mechanism candidates

The fixed baseline is retained as the comparator. Only these three escalation
candidates may enter a ready comparison:

1. dynamic local software auto-framing with no physical aim mechanism;
2. the smallest bounded, locking manual tilt mechanism; and
3. motorized aim, explicitly last resort.

Each candidate requires an exact implementation and control-plane digest.
Software auto-framing may not expand capture authority, hide crop or floor
loss, use network inference, or infer that a person consented to tracking.
Manual adjustment must preserve shutter and indicator visibility, cable
strain relief, stability, calibration invalidation and safe reach. Motorized
aim additionally requires obstruction, pinch, unexpected-motion, acoustic,
power, position-truth and fail-safe evidence.

## Common remediation matrix

Every alternative runs against the same frozen fixed-placement failure ledger
and the same complete blocking persona, zone-point and motion grid. Each exact
candidate configuration has 50 blocking cells:

- school-age-child-standing and adult-standing;
- center plus four zone-edge points; and
- neutral full-body, jump, duck, dodge-left and dodge-right motions.

Each cell requires 20 valid scheduled trials. Configuration, cell and trial
counts remain null until the complete failure ledger and exact candidate
settings are frozen. One mechanism, room, persona, zone, motion, retry or later
success cannot rescue another failed or missing cell.

## Burden, lifecycle and fault evidence

Each alternative has 24 independently closed evidence domains covering
coverage repair, privacy, security, disclosure, installation, calibration,
reach, accessibility, stale-state handling, cost, noise, power, thermal load,
thickness, mass, part count, manufacturing, durability, reliability,
serviceability, repair, replacement, update behavior and end-of-life handling.
That is 72 candidate-domain cells. A weighted score or attractive aggregate
cannot hide a failed safety, privacy, coverage or reliability domain.

The lifecycle/fault matrix crosses three candidates, ten lifecycle
transitions and ten faults. Twenty valid cycles per cell produce 300 cells and

## Human-use evidence

If separately authorized, the human-use matrix crosses three candidates, five
persona classes and six common tasks at 20 valid trials per cell: 90 cells and
1,800 trials. It measures whether people can understand framing and privacy
state, complete calibration, obtain usable framing, recover after a placement
or player change, stop/revoke capture and recover a declared fault without
unsafe reach or hidden assistance.

Task completion cannot hide false privacy beliefs, discomfort, assistance,
unsafe zone entry, inaccessible controls or failed recovery. Child, adult,
limited-range, low-vision and color-vision evidence remains separate.

## Decision boundary

thickness, cost and reliability gates remain null until frozen before any
alternative operation. Zero-tolerance gates already forbid safety/privacy
