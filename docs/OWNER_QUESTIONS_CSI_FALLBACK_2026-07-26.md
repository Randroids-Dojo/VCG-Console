# Owner questions: Pi 5 CSI fallback — 2026-07-26

These questions block I-033/I-034 execution and selection. They do not block
shared-UVC qualification, software capture work, fixture design, or validation
of the strict zero-result plan.

## CSI-001: exact UVC trigger

Which shared-UVC gate, repeated failure count, time window and final-product
environment are sufficient to open Q-013?

Safe default: no fallback opening from specifications, cable convenience,
driver availability, a single run, another target, or an aggregate.

## CSI-002: trigger review and root cause

What reproduction, fixture controls, first-failure preservation and root-cause
confidence are required before testing a different camera path?

Safe default: a harness-invalid event or unfrozen environment is not a product
failure. Preserve the exact UVC failure rather than repairing its ledger.

## CSI-003: exact candidate identity

Which Camera Module 3 Wide part, revision, lens variant, supplier, received
units/lots, ribbon, connector and accessories define the candidate?

Safe default: candidate family and advertised 120-degree diagonal field of
view do not authorize purchase or generalize one received unit.

## CSI-004: matched attribution

Is a pure CSI-interface claim required, and if so what matched sensor, optics,
lens, controls and processing comparison can establish it?

Safe default: report only the complete camera-path difference. Do not attribute
Camera Module 3 Wide sensor/optics results to CSI alone.

## CSI-005: final target and integration

Which exact Pi, OS/image, libcamera/Picamera stack, AI accelerator, power,
cooling, storage, enclosure, camera placement, ribbon route, shutter and
indicator define the comparison?

Safe default: use the final complete Pi workload and integrated enclosure
direction. A bench preview cannot qualify packaging or service.

## CSI-006: capture modes and timestamp authority

Must CSI meet the unchanged D-044 1920x1080@60 gate? Which controls, buffers,
clock domains and exposure authority define glass-to-memory timing?

Safe default: retain D-044 and D-110. Capture arrival and inference-only timing
are not exposure authority; any relaxed mode needs a separate decision.

## CSI-007: material closure

What minimum improvement on the exact failed UVC gate is material enough to
justify a second capture path, and what regressions are acceptable?

Safe default: freeze the trigger-specific improvement and every common
regression threshold before viewing comparison results.

## CSI-008: samples and coverage

How many units, lots, repetitions, soak hours, lighting strata, room
placements, personas, edge positions, postures, motions, faults and recovery
cycles are required?

Safe default: inherit every applicable upstream campaign. A 16-cell phase
summary cannot replace physical per-cell coverage.

## CSI-009: privacy, safety and service

What optical shutter, indicator, disablement, ribbon isolation/retention,
strain, ESD, opening, replacement, recalibration and household-safety evidence
is mandatory?

Safe default: same or stricter D-045/privacy/recovery gates with zero rescue.
An integrated ribbon path must remain replaceable and fail safely.

## CSI-010: maintenance and product complexity

What delivered-cost, enclosure-volume, mass, installation time, service time,
driver/update burden, calibration burden and cross-tier divergence are
acceptable?

Safe default: price and latency alone cannot justify a second capture path;
count its complete release, support, recovery and documentation surface.

## CSI-011: ranking, expiry and retest

What decision weights, tie-breaks, evidence expiry and revision/firmware/lens/
driver retest rules apply?

Safe default: keep them null until review and scope any eventual approval to
the exact received identity and frozen evidence.

## CSI-012: authority

Who may purchase or return hardware, contact vendors, install/reroute ribbons,
mutate drivers or firmware, operate cameras/targets, collect participants,
write calibration, select CSI, change the BOM, publish claims or supersede
D-043?

Safe default: none of those actions is authorized by the plan. A positive
comparison informs a separate explicit owner decision.
