# Recommended FDM print specification

## Shared settings

- Process: FDM/FFF
- Nozzle: 0.4 mm
- Material: PETG
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Perimeters/walls: 3
- Infill: 20% gyroid or a comparable isotropic pattern
- Supports: off unless the selected slicer identifies a specific unsupported internal feature
- Premium finishing: none

## Bottom enclosure

- Color: black
- Quantity: 1
- Preserve the model's supplied orientation where practical

## Branded lid

- Color: black
- Quantity: 1
- Print in the same orientation as the original WrenchWorks3D profile, with the exterior presentation face toward the build plate
- The 1.0 mm logo cavity is shallow and remains over 1.5 mm of lid wall

## Logo insert

- Color: red
- Quantity: 1
- Print flat
- At 0.9 mm thick, normal top/bottom shell settings make the insert effectively solid even when the service quotes nominal 20% infill

Dry-fit the insert before adhesive. The model provides 0.25 mm clearance per side and 0.10 mm total depth clearance. If adhesion is desired after the dry fit, use small dots of a PETG-compatible flexible adhesive rather than flooding the recess.
